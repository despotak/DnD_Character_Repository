## Was the woman the Srinshee?

What Panagiotis wrote about her (ELF, Fragments I–IX): tall, pale, dark hair, "something
red upon her lips"; beautiful and frightening at once. The old elf at the desk had "far
too little blood". She said "You are late" / "No. Don't." / "Not yet". She hummed the
three notes. You couldn't kill her. She asked you something; you answered; she said
"Good." She touched the leaf and whispered "Dahast". "No. But I knew the woman who taught
her mother." "An elf does not call another elf. He leaves something behind that can be
remembered."

What 2e canon says about the Srinshee (*Cormanthyr* CEoE, *The Fall of Myth Drannor*
FoMD):

**For it:**
- **She was Alea Dahast's friend** (CEoE p. 120). Alea, the High Lady of Art, is the
  likeliest "woman who taught her mother", whether "her" means Elyndra or Elyndra's
  mother. Someone who knew Alea well would say exactly that sentence.
- **Pale:** ivory skin (CEoE p. 122).
- **An elf**, and very old: once a baelnorn (−712 to 261 DR), then reborn young-looking
  (CEoE p. 122; FoMD p. 4). Old enough to have known four Dahast generations.
- **"Not yet":** in 666 DR she took the Ruler's Blade, refused to be Coronal, and
  promised to return only when the dream was truly the people's own and she was truly
  wanted and needed (FoMD pp. 5–7). A woman who waits for the right moment, and says so.
- **High Magic:** Grand Mage, one of only two who know the Sundering. Blurred memories,
  a sword lowered without deciding: well within her reach.
- **The Garden:** an Elder of the Windsong Tower, who studied the Golden Grove for a
  year (CEoE pp. 122, 159).
- She has worn only black or midnight blue since 390 DR, in mourning (CEoE p. 39).

**Against it:**
- **Her hair.** Sapphire blue, and nearly two feet longer than she is tall (CEoE
  p. 122). "Dark hair" could be dark blue in a bad memory, but that much hair is hard to
  forget.
- **"Tall."** 2e never gives her height; she calls her own shoulders slight (FoMD p. 6).
- **Red lips and too little blood.** The vampire reading doesn't fit a lawful good Grand
  Mage. Unless the red was something else, and the blood was someone else's doing.
- **The red feather and the perfume** at the Oghmanite's near-deaths, if that's the same
  woman: nothing in her canon matches.
- In 2e she vanished in 666 with the Blade. (3e lore has her return in 1377 DR.) In
  Panagiotis's timeline, anything goes.

**What to watch for in play, silently:** blue in anyone's hair, the Ruler's Blade, the
Diamond Tower, Alea's name in a stranger's mouth, a woman who calls him "Dahast" before
he's said it.

## The mark's source

Panagiotis's birthmark image looks like the sigil from *The Elder Scrolls IV: **Oblivion***: two
horns and a central drop. The mark's dwarven name, *Ruan Famkul*, means "that which has
been deliberately removed from remembrance". In other words, **oblivion**. If that's a
joke, it's a very good one. Whether he meant anything by the source, we keep to
ourselves.
