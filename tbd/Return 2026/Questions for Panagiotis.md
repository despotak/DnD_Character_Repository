## 4. Ideas I'd like to add

All optional. Take what fits your plans and drop the rest.

- **Idea: the elf goes by "Dahast".** He has no name, so he answers to the one thing
  that is his: the house. His mother loved "a single surviving name"; he is one.
  Outsiders take it for a personal name and he lets them. The true name stays under the
  mark until you decide otherwise. I'd rather not know in advance.
- **Idea: Dispel Falsehood as the key to the elf's lost memories.** The Shield of the
  Scroll's *Dispel Falsehood* works against "spells meant to erase/alter memory (like
  the *Forget* spell)". The elf's Fragments I–IX look like exactly that kind of damage.
  I'd love the moment when one of my characters gets a piece of the other's memory
  back to be a real beat in play. Is that within what the power can do, and could some
  pieces be beyond it?
- **Idea: let the two meet as strangers.** The Oghmanite was told "Find the reader …
  ask him what he remembers"; the elf, "Seek the one who remembers". I'd rather they
  find that out at the table than start as old friends.
- **Idea: how the elf reaches the party.** He's bound for Waterdeep, and the party
  is in Westgate. Either Khelben, once he has the letter, sends him on to the Westgate
  Harpers, or he hears of the Teziir dreamer's chained man and goes east on his own.
- **Idea: elven chain as a gift.** A bladesinger who wants to cast in armour needs
  elven chain, and it can't be bought. Orendyl's own, given when the elf left Mir,
  would make a good start.
- **Idea: the bladesinger's duty, and the woman.** The kit obliges him to aid "any
  elf in need, at risk of life and limb, unless that elf is a proven enemy". The woman
  speaks as an elf. He lowered his sword and doesn't know why. Whether she counts as a
  "proven enemy" is a question he'll have to answer in play.
- **Idea: the Oghmanite's family as copyists or bookbinders.** He'd be a craftsman of
  the book before he was its scholar, which is why he can date a manuscript by its
  binding.
- **Idea: his mentor and the Society of the Moon.** The old party knew Father
  Crandam Ethander, Chief of the Oghmanite clergy, among the keepers of the Legion's
  secret in Dancing Vale. If my mentor was Crandam, or one of his people, my interest
  in the Nine stops being a coincidence.
- **Idea: a short scene for the first Corvus encounter.** One paragraph would do.
  "You survived, but you did not understand him" deserves a beginning.
- **Idea: physical marks.** Both hands scarred from pulling the Ledger out of the
  ritual fire; the thigh wound from Asbravn aches in the cold.
