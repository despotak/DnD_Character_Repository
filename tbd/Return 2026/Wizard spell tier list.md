# AD&D 2nd Edition wizard spells: a tier list

Every wizard spell in the *Player's Handbook* (revised printing, 1995), **312 spells**, grouped by level and ranked into tiers. The list is Appendix 1 (pp. 164–165) and the spell text is Appendix 3 (pp. 170–251). The casting time and saving throw columns are copied from the book, and every number in a note was checked against that spell's text.

## Scope

- **PHB only.** Panagiotis hasn't said which other books he allows. The [all-books list](<Wizard spell tier list - all books/00 Index.md>) ranks the other 2,197 wizard spells against these same tiers.
- **Tiers are within the level.** S at 1st level means "one of the best 1st-level spells", not "as good as *wish*".
- **The reference player is a generalist mage** in an ordinary campaign of dungeons, wilderness and intrigue, judged from the level the spell becomes castable through the next five or so levels.
- 📖 marks a spell in the bladesinger's spellbook or on his daily list. His own read is at the end.

| Tier | Meaning |
|:--:|---|
| **S** | Defines the level. Take it whenever you can and memorize it most days. |
| **A** | Excellent. Earns a slot on most adventuring days, or is a must-have in the book. |
| **B** | Good, but situational, or outshone by a spell doing a similar job. |
| **C** | Niche. Real uses, but you rarely memorize it. |
| **D** | Rarely worth a slot. Odd cases or flavor. |
| **F** | A trap, or close to useless as written. |

## What moves a ranking in 2e

1. **Saving throws depend on the target, not the caster.** There is no spell DC, so a "save negates" spell gets worse as enemies level up. Spells with **no save** (*magic missile*), or no save under a Hit Dice or hit-point cap (*sleep*, *cloudkill*, *death spell*, the *power words*), and spells that impose a **save penalty**, rank higher than they would in later editions.
2. **Casting time is an initiative penalty, and a hit before the spell goes off spoils it.** A casting time of 1 is worth a great deal. Anything that takes a round or more is not a combat spell. Per `Spell lists.md`, a bladesinger adds +2 to every casting time.
3. **Walls allow no save.** *Wall of fire*, *wall of stone*, *wall of force* and *forcecage* keep working against monsters whose saves are too good for anything aimed at them.
4. **The text's costs count.** *Haste* ages its targets a year, *teleport* has an error table, *wish* ages the caster five years, *find familiar* can kill its caster, and many spells eat expensive components.
5. **Slots are scarce.** A 9th-level mage has 4/3/3/2/1 and no bonus spells for Intelligence, so a spell that ends an encounter beats one that chips at it.
6. **Utility counts as much as combat.** Getting in, out or past something, finding things out, and shutting out a whole class of threat all rank high even with no combat use.
7. **Illusions are strong but lean on the DM's ruling.** The notes say so wherever it matters.
