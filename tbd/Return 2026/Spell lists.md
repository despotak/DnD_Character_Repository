# Spell lists, at about 350,000–400,000 XP

AD&D 2e. The lists below are built from the *Player's Handbook* (PHB); Panagiotis hasn't
said which other books he allows. Levels are worked out from the PHB experience tables;
Panagiotis has no level limits.

| | XP | Level | Spells per day |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bladesinger** (fighter/mage: XP split evenly, 175–200K each) | 350–400K | **Fighter 8 / Mage 9** | Mage 9: **4 / 3 / 3 / 2 / 1** (1st to 5th) |
| **Oghmanite** (Shield of the Scroll, paladin XP table) | 350–400K | **9** (10th needs 600K) | Priest spells from 9th: **one 1st-level spell** |

Wizards get no bonus spells for Intelligence, and paladins none for Wisdom.

## The bladesinger: a mage of 9th level

**How he casts.** Bladesinger rules (CBoE): he casts one-handed at +2 casting time,
can't attack in a round he casts, and gains AC against melee while casting. So the list
leans on spells cast **before** a fight or in a quick gap: protection, movement, and a
few that end a fight. Thaelith taught him magic as conversation, so there's divination and
language too, and his people's "not to be found" gives him concealment. Casting times
below are the book's; add 2 for him.

### Before asking for a single spell

Every spell he asks for needs a roll under his Intelligence's *chance to learn spell*
(PHB p. 22, Table 4). **A failed roll locks that spell out until he reaches 10th level as
a mage.** His Int isn't rolled yet. The kit minimum is 15, and a gold elf counts as a high
elf, so there's no racial bonus.

| Int | Chance to learn | Most spells per spell level (optional rule) |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | 65% | 11 |
| 16 | 70% | 11 |
| 17 | 75% | 14 |
| 18 | 85% | 18 |

Two questions for Panagiotis come first, because they change the list more than any pick:

1. **Does he use the optional limit on spells per level?** At Int 15–16 it's 11 per spell
   level.
2. **Does the starting book need rolls** (Questions #14), or only the spells added later?
   And which books beyond the PHB does he allow?

At 70%, asking for 30 spells gets about 21 and locks out about 9. So the lists below are
ranked: ask from the top, and ask the backup only if the spell it backs up fails.

### What changed from the old plan

- **His elven chain makes armor useless and shield situational.** The chain is AC 5 before
  Dexterity. *Armor* gives AC 6 and fails on anyone already at AC 6 or better. *Shield*
  gives AC 4 against attacks from the front, so it's worth a point at most there (whether
  Dex adds to it is a DM call). Its value is AC 3 against arrows, AC 2 against thrown
  weapons, and blocking *magic missile*.
- **Haste costs him almost nothing.** It ages the target a year, and Panagiotis's elves live
  800–1000 years.
- **The +2 splits the book in two.** Spells cast before the fight can be slow. Spells cast
  during it should stay at 3 or less after the +2. Casting time 5 becomes 7 for him, so
  *hold monster*, *cone of cold* and *wall of force* open a fight; they don't rescue one.

## The Oghmanite: Shield of the Scroll, 9th level

**What he gets.** At 9th level he casts **one 1st-level priest spell a day**. His spheres
under the kit: **All, Charm, Combat, Divination, Protection** (no Healing). He can't use
priest scrolls. His real tools at this level are the kit powers:

- **Detect Deception/Illusion**, 3 times a day.
- **Dispel Falsehood**, using the turning table as a 7th-level cleric (paladins turn as a
  cleric two levels lower).
- **Legend Lore**: 5% per level, so **45%** at 9th.

### His one daily spell (1st-level priest, from his spheres)

| Spell | Sphere | When |
|---|---|---|
| ***Detect poison*** | Divination | In a library: "some books bite" |
| ***Sanctuary*** | Protection | His second duty is to protect scholars and witnesses who can't defend themselves; cast it on them |
| ***Protection from evil*** | Protection | In the field, against Corvus and whatever serves him |
| ***Command*** | Charm | Interrogation without a lie |
| ***Remove fear*** | Charm | For the archivists and acolytes he gets out alive |
| ***Bless*** | All | Before a fight |
| ***Detect magic***, ***detect evil*** | Divination | Investigation |
| ***Purify food and drink*** | All | Plague villages, like the one where he saved the Ledger |
| ***Endure heat/cold*** | Protection | The northern winters Lexicon is built for |

**Default:** *detect poison* in cities and libraries, *sanctuary* or *protection from evil*
on the road.

### What comes next

From the PHB paladin table: **10th**: two 1st-level · **11th**: 2 / 1 · **12th**: 2 / 2 ·
**13th**: 2 / 2 / 1.

Good 2nd-level picks in his spheres when they arrive: *augury* and *know alignment*
(Divination), *enthrall* (Charm), *spiritual hammer* (Combat), *withdraw* (All). For a
Shield the obvious one is ***know alignment***. It isn't a lie detector, but it's close
to the right question.
