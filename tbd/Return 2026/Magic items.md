# Magic items: options for Panagiotis

Panagiotis asked what magic items I have in mind. Here are **four packages per
character**, from light to overpowered: pick one, mix, or trim. All items are from the
2e *Dungeon Master's Guide* (DMG) unless marked; the exact mechanics are his book's.
Every item has a reason in the backgrounds.

The party has **no healer** (the Shield's kit dropped all healing). Items marked ✚ help
with that.

---

## The bladesinger

Already has: **Rathael**, the Moon Between Blades. **Elven chain** is still open (Questions #10).

### Light

- ✚ **Potions of healing** ×2
- **Cloak of elvenkind**: a parting gift from Sarenestar Ael'Vala ("we survived because
  we learned not to be found")
- **Two spell scrolls**: *comprehend languages*, *identify*
- A **lap harp**, not magical: the Dahast arms are a harp and a dagger

*Effect:* he leans entirely on Rathael and his chain. Fragile, and that fits a young elf
newly out of the forest.

### Reasonable (my preference)

- Everything in Light, plus:
- **Boots of elvenkind**, so the pair is complete
- **Amulet of proof against detection and location**: his people's creed in metal, and
  something has been finding him since the Garden
- **One ioun stone**, circling his head like a small star for the boy called "Little
  Star":
  - **pink rhomboid** (+1 Con, offsetting the elf's −1), or
  - **dusty rose prism** (+1 protection)
- ✚ A third **potion of healing**

*Effect:* stealthy, hard to find, a little tougher. Still nothing that solves problems
for him.

### Strong

- Everything in Reasonable, plus:
- **Ring of free action**: nothing holds a bladesinger's feet
- **Cloak of displacement** instead of the cloak of elvenkind: harder to hit, at the
  cost of stealth
- A second ioun stone, the **pale lavender ellipsoid**, which absorbs spells up to
  4th level until it burns out
- **Ring of wizardry** (for example, the variant that doubles 1st-level spells)

*Effect:* a front-line duellist who can shrug off a spell or two. Encounters need
tuning up.

### OP

- Everything in Strong, plus:
- **Alea Dahast's selû'kiira** from the start (*Cormanthyr* pp. 152–153), instead of
  as a hook in play
- **Ring of spell turning**
- **Boots of speed** instead of the boots of elvenkind
- The **pale green prism** ioun stone (+1 level)

*Effect:* he outclasses most threats. The selû'kiira in particular spends one of the
best hooks before the story earns it. Not recommended; listed so the ceiling is clear.

## The Oghmanite

No 10-item limit (Panagiotis). He tithes his wealth, so these are tools of the order, not
riches.

### Light

- ✚ **Keoghtom's ointment**, one jar: healing, poison and disease. The Seat keeps a jar
  for its Shields
- **Eyes of minute seeing**: **his mentor's inherited lenses**, for reading ink and paper
- **Periapt of proof against poison**: "some books bite"

*Effect:* a scholar's kit. His sword is only steel.

### Reasonable (my preference)

- Everything in Light, plus:
- **Helm of comprehending languages and reading magic**, worn as a plain circlet if
  allowed: dead languages and spell-texts
- **Phylactery of faithfulness**: warns him before an act would break his vows. A
  conscience he can consult, for a man under the Vow of Truth
- **Long sword +1**, from the Seat's armoury

*Effect:* the investigator the background describes, without making the mysteries easy.

### Strong

- Everything in Reasonable, plus:
- ✚ **Periapt of wound closure**: the Asbravn wound, never again
- **Lantern of revealing**: invisible thieves and hidden rooms
- **Heward's handy haversack**: for everything he collects (the kit's 15 lb of reference
  books stays put by its own rule)
- **Pearl of wisdom** (+1 Wis)
- **Sword +1, +3 vs. lycanthropes and shape changers** instead of the plain +1: Corvus
  wears faces
- For **Lexicon**: **horseshoes of speed**

*Effect:* a capable field agent with real healing back-up. The lantern starts to blunt
illusion-based mysteries.

### OP

- Everything in Strong, plus:
- **Gem of seeing**: true seeing at will
- **Robe of eyes**
- **Medallion of ESP**
- ✚ **Ring of regeneration**

*Effect:* he sees through the woman, the mark, the Redactors and every disguise in one
go, and almost can't die. Not recommended; it flattens exactly the kind of mystery both
backgrounds are built on.

## Either way

The best prizes are better found than given: Alea's selû'kiira, whatever wakes "the apex
of Dahast" in Rathael, and the Golden Grove's knowledge. Those stay in the hooks.
