# Email handouts

Everything in-game from the emails involving Panayotis Galanopoulos in `despotak@gmail.com`: 259 messages, January 2014 to
March 2019. Attachments are saved as sent, in one folder per email, with file
timestamps set to the send date. Email text is saved as Markdown with quoted replies
removed. The rest of the 259 messages is scheduling.

## Three campaigns, two DMs

The same table ran two campaigns in alternation, with different DMs. The Realms game
was not new in 2014: on 1 April Savvas writes «να γυρίσουμε στου Παναγιώτη», to *go
back* to Panagiotis's. The earliest Realms-specific email is from **23 February 2014**,
when Panagiotis asks only Christos, Anthie and Savvas to send their character builds,
«έστω και draft» (even as a draft).

| Campaign | DM | In the mailbox | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Forgotten Realms**, the Dunendain game | Panagiotis | Feb 2014 → Apr 2015 | `Forgotten Realms (Panagiotis)/` |
| **Gerendosch**, Savvas's homebrew world (Akh Abar, Elarn) | Savvas | Jan → May 2014 | `Gerendosch (Savvas)/` |
| **Planescape**, a separate game in Sigil | Panagiotis | Mar 2019, pitched to Christos only | `Planescape 2019 (Panagiotis)/` |

**Timeline, from the emails:**

- **Jan–Mar 2014: Gerendosch.** The party searches Akh Abar for "the box" and plans to
  leave westward. The campaign is to be paused once they leave the city. Nestoras
  proposes retiring Diarmuid from the party, and Savvas agrees.
- **Apr 2014: back to the Realms.** On 13 April Savvas leaves Gerendosch "εκεί που το
  αφήσαμε" (where we left it). On 12 May one pending Gerendosch item was to be played
  out first, and then the Realms game would continue.
- **Apr–Jul 2014: the Realms, full table.** Yuri's notes, the Journals and scrolls, and
  Sylvia's account of the Spectral Legion.
- **Aug 2014.** The full table has stopped at **Hidden Valley**.
- **Sep 2014 → Apr 2015: the Realms, small group.** Christos and Nestoras continue, and
  Evi is invited. Sylvia's briefings on the Forest of Mir and on Sigil follow. On
  3 February 2015 Panagiotis asks Nestoras to bring «το κουτί του Planescape», the
  Planescape box.
- **31 Mar 2015, last recorded position.** Back from **Pandemonium**, on the way to talk
  with **Estevan**. Christos's plan: follow Estevan through the third door, because the
  party still lacks what it was promised for the **Armory**. The next session was
  swapped for a coffee on 1 April 2015, and the mail goes quiet after that.
- **2015 → 2026, outside the mailbox.** Christos stopped playing; Panagiotis, Savvas and
  others carried on. In 2026 only Panagiotis and Savvas remain at the table, and
  Christos is coming back with two new characters, an elven bladesinger and a human
  Shield of the Scroll of Oghma. So the Pandemonium position above is where *Christos's*
  thread ended in 2015, not where the campaign stands now. Eleven years of play happened
  without him, and none of it is in this mailbox.

Cover notes on the attachments, as sent:

- **Journals:** texts the party found the night before. «σας στέλνω τα κείμενα που
  βρίκατε χθες (Ανθή θα προσπαθήσω να σου φτιάξω και την μετεφρασμένη έκδοση)»
- **Scrolls:** the rest of the scrolls, plus a correction that matters for reading the
  two journals: **they are not continuous.** Each paragraph is a separate fragment from
  a ransacked room. «τα 2 ημερολόγια δεν είναι ενιαία!! αυτά που βρίκατε είναι κομμάτια
  και μάλιστα κάθε παράγραφος είναι ένα κομμάτι. δεν είναι συνεχόμενα μεταξύ τους. είναι
  ότι βρίκατε στην ανακατοσούρα του δωματίου!!» Only `Ελιές 3` was attached, and no
  `Ελιές 1` or `2` exists anywhere in the mailbox. The mail doesn't say whether the 3
  marks a part or a revision.
- **Spectral Legion:** «είναι αυτά που σας είχε πει η Sylvia...» An 11:20 send went out
  with no attachment; this is the 11:21 resend.
- **Forest of Mir:** «info για Forest of Mir attached.»
- **Sigil:** Sylvia's general briefing. The details were to come from the relevant
  contact once the party reached the city. «Περισσότερες λεπτομέριες σας είπε ότι θα
  μάθετε εκεί όταν με το καλό έρθετε σε επαφή με τον αρμόδιο Συνεργάτη.»
