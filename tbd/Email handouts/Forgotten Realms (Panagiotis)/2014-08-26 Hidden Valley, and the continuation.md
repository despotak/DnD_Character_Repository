# Hidden Valley, and the continuation

Two messages from the `FR Campaign` thread. In August 2014 the full table had stopped at **Hidden Valley**, and Panagiotis asked for questions about the journey so he could prepare answers. In September the game continued with a smaller group (Christos, Nestoras, with Evi invited): Panagiotis calls it "τη γαμάτη ιδέα μου για την συνέχιση του Campaign" (my awesome idea for continuing the campaign). The Forest of Mir and Sigil handouts that followed went only to that group. Quoted replies removed; text as sent.
