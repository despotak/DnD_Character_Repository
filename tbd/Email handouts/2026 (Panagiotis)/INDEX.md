## His answers, 10-09 (digest)

**Elf: rules**
- **No middle age.** In his campaign dwarves live 300–500 years and elves 800–1000; 230
  is a young adult. Middle age applies after 400.
- **Proficiencies:** not CBoE's +1 per decade. He gives more NWPs generally (languages
  included), mainly from the **Int and Wis** bonuses, plus some extra for age. Bring a
  list and agree it in person.
- **Gold elf counts as a high elf** (so +1 Dex / −1 Con).
- **No level limits** in his campaigns.

**Elf: background**
- **The expedition:** following the star was your own thing. You went alone, inside a
  wider mission, because it suited you. Names and leader to be decided. **Few know you
  came back changed; the leader knows, and was the only other one to return alive.**
- **The three notes:** the dream and the Stanza are different things, and the Stanza's
  "last note" is a **fourth**. Whether the two orders differ, or should, is **your
  conclusion to draw**.
- **Thaelith's "departure"** was her death: the passing to Evermeet.
- **930 and 714:** Myth Drannor fell as in canon. "Year of Doom" refers to that, **not**
  to your birth. **Your mother fled Myth Drannor at its fall**, not in 930. **930 is
  when you were born.**
- **Khelben:** you know he's a Chosen of Mystra, the widely known facts (Waterdeep and
  so on), and where to find him.

**Oghmanite: background**
- **Two final notes**, separate. Treat both as last notes.
- "Last death" was a **typo**.
- **The Nine:** you don't know the name "Spectral Legion" at all. Only what's in the
  background.
- **Crandam Ethander:** you know his name and position. You don't know of any link
  with your order. **Your mentor, the gnome**, would naturally know him.
- **The Ledger of Grace** was at **the church in Berdusk** for a while, then sent to
  **Candlekeep**. You never read the names: there was no reason to. Open to discussion
  if you have an idea.
- **The red rain:** not the same event. The rain after Karran-Kul is recent and widely
  known, mostly as an omen and a superstition. Elanith is ancient history, hardly known.
- **Bhaal:** the shards story is known in religious circles and to the Harpers (the
  details, not widely). **That Bhaal was "dead" and now isn't is known to many, even
  common folk.** The night of his return shook the world. Quick Cat: unknown, except
  perhaps in specific circles.

**World**
- **Myth Drannor and the Trio Nefarious:** as in canon. **Netheril:** as in canon.
- Whether you know **when Bhaal was killed** in the distant past: a percentile roll.
- **Dancing Vale = Hidden Valley = canon's Dancing Place.** That's what he based it on.
  Whether your order has people there you don't know (you can ask). **You have never
  been there.**
