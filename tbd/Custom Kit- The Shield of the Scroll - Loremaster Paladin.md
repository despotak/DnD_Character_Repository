**<u>Custom Kit: The Shield of the Scroll (Loremaster “Paladin”)</u>**

Στον κόσμο των Forgotten Realms, η Εκκλησία του Oghma αυστηρά σημειώνει (επίσημο lore) ότι "*the church sponsors no military or knightly orders*". Όμως, επίσης αυστηρά διαδίδει την προστασία της σε διάφορα «***scholarly fellowships and monkish orders»***. Η σκέψη μου λοιπόν είναι ότι μπορούμε να «κουμπώσουμε» το χαρακτήρα σε ένα «στρατιωτικό παράρτημα» ή «εσωτερικό champion» των «**The Children of the Passive Voice»**—ενός order από ‘γνώστες’ οι οποίοι προστατεύουν βιβλιοθήκες, κρυμμένες γραφές, απόκρυφες γνώσεις και χαμένα μοναστήρια/αβαεία κλπ. Ο χαρακτήρας αυτός, θα μπορούσε να λειτουργεί ως "Shield of the Scroll," ένας ‘holy guardian’ με σκοπό την ανάκτηση χαμένης γνώσης, αλλά και τη προστασία της γνώσης και όσων τη φυλάσσουν, γενικά.

**Custom Kit: The Shield of the Scroll (Loremaster «Paladin»)**

**Deity:** Oghma

**Affiliated Lore Order:** Internal militant champion of *The Children of the Passive Voice*

**Alignment Exception:** Βασικά πρέπει να είναι Lawful Neutral ή και Neutral.

Ως **Ασπίδα του Κειμηλίου** (*Shield of the Scroll*) είναι ένας εξειδικευμένος ιερός πολεμιστής που θεωρεί την άγνοια ως ασθένεια και την καταστροφή της γνώσης ως πράξη απόλυτης κακίας. Αυτοί οι «paladins», ταξιδεύουν όπου χρειάζεται, όπως σε σκοτεινά ερείπια, για να ανακτήσουν χαμένα κείμενα, να προστατεύσουν εστίες μάθησης και να επιβάλουν την αντικειμενική διάδοση της αλήθειας. Είναι εξίσου ικανοί στη χρήση νεκρών ή και αρχαίων διαλέκτων όσο και φονικοί με το long sword (*το αγαπημένο όπλο του Oghma*)

**Requirements**

**Minimum Attributes:** Strength 12, Intelligence 14, Wisdom 14, Charisma 13. *(το υψηλό Intelligence ταιριάζει με το ακαδημαϊκό focus.*

**Weapon Proficiencies: Required:** Longsword.

**Allowed:** Any, but they are encouraged to favor weapons that can disable or subdue, as well as classic scholarly options like the quarterstaff or mace.

**Non-Weapon Proficiencies: Bonus (Free):** Reading/Writing (Common or Native), Ancient History, 1 Ancient Language.

**Recommended:** Ancient Languages, Religion, Heraldry, Spellcraft, Local History, Poetry.

**Special Benefits: Legend Lore (As a Bard):** Through exhaustive study at Oghma's high temples, the character can identify the history and properties of magical items, ancient ruins, or legendary figures. (*This behaves exactly like the standard 2e Bard's ability, starting at a base 5% chance per level to know something about a legendary object or place*).

**The Mind's Fortress:** The Paladin gains a **+2 bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting magic** (such as *Charm*, *Hypnotism*, *Forget*, or memory modification spells) and illusions designed to obscure the truth.

**Turn Undead Variance:** Instead of turning undead as a lower-level cleric, the paladin instead uses the ability to **"Dispel Falsehood."** By forcefully invoking Oghma's name, they can attempt to clear magical illusions, break a magical *Charm* on an ally, or counter sound-based magic (such as a harpy's song) within a 30-foot radius. The success rate uses the standard Paladin Turning table against the spell caster's effective level.

**Special Hindrances: The Vow of Truth:** The Paladin is forbidden from telling a deliberate lie or actively spreading misinformation. If they are forced to protect a dangerous secret, they must rely on clever diplomacy, silence, or a refusal to answer. Intentionally destroying a book, scroll, or piece of unique history causes an immediate loss of all paladin abilities until an *Atonement* spell is cast by a High Priest of Oghma.

**The Tithe of Lore:** (*The standard 2e Paladin requirement to donate excess wealth is modified*). The Shield of the Scroll may keep coin only to maintain their armor, weapon, and basic survival. All other treasure **must be spent on acquiring rare books, mapping undiscovered ruins, or funding scribes and libraries** affiliated with *The Children of the Passive Voice*.

**The Weighted Pack:** To facilitate their research, the paladin must always carry a heavy collection of reference texts, inks, parchment, and journaling tools. This automatically adds a flat **15 lbs of immovable encumbrance** to their gear, regardless of how light they try to travel.

**❌ Powers Removed (What you DO NOT have)**

**Lay on Hands (Healing):** Lost. Your character is a guardian of the mind and history, not a traditional battlefield medic.

**Cure Disease:** Lost. You do not possess the divine medical grace to cleanse physical plagues.

**Protection From Evil Aura:** Lost. You do not project a passive 10-foot radius penalty to evil attackers.

**Turning Undead:** Lost. You do not turn skeletons or zombies like a standard cleric.

**Powers Modified (Altered for Oghma)**

**Detect Evil → Detect Deception/Illusion:** Instead of sensing general moral evil, you can use this ability up to 3 times a day to scan a person or an object within 60 feet. It reveals if a person is **telling a deliberate lie**, if a document is a **forgery**, or if a magical **illusion** is obscuring a physical space or text.

**Turn Undead → Dispel Falsehood:** At 3rd level, you gain the ability to forcefully invoke Oghma's name to shatter mental manipulation. This acts as a targeted *Dispel Magic*, but it works **exclusively** against charms, mind-control, phantasms, illusions, or spells meant to erase/alter memory (like the *Forget* spell). You roll on the standard Paladin Turning Table using the spellcaster's level as the target "Undead" type.

**Powers Retained (What you KEEP identically)**

**Immunity to All Diseases:** Retained. Sifting through ancient, moldy tombs and sealed, plagued ruins requires an ironclad immune system.

**+2 to All Saving Throws:** Retained. Your unshakeable devotion to cosmic truth keeps your spirit anchored against all forms of magical and physical assault.

**The Paladin’s Mount:** Retained. You still receive your divinely gifted companion (your Silver-Maned Boreal Destrier) at 4th level.

**Clerical Spell Casting:** Retained. At 9th level, you gain the ability to cast priest spells. However, your sphere access is restricted to Oghma's portfolios: **All, Charm, Combat, Divination, and Protection.**

**Scenario Breakdown: "Detect Deception / Illusion" in Action:** This ability replaces *Detect Evil*. It requires **one round of total concentration**, during which you can take no other actions. It functions up to **3 times per day**, reaching out in a 60-foot cone.

**1. The Social Encounter: Cross-Examining a Zhentarim Spy**

Imagine your Paladin is interrogating a captured merchant in a tavern, who is suspected of smuggling stolen historical archives to Zhentil Keep.

**Round 1 (Activation):** You stand perfectly still, staring at the suspect. You invoke your divine sense.

**The DM’s Description:** *“As you focus, the air around the merchant shifts. When he speaks his name and claims to be a simple silk merchant from Sembia, his words trigger a physical reaction in your divine sight. A faint, oily black smoke seems to seep from his mouth, and the edges of his clothing briefly flicker with a strange refraction.”*

**The Mechanical Result:** You instantly know two things. First, his claim about his identity is a **deliberate lie** (indicated by the smoke). Second, his physical appearance is being altered by a minor magical **illusion** or disguise kit (indicated by the refraction). You didn't read his mind to get the true answer, but you successfully stripped away his deception, giving you massive leverage for your next interrogation check or a *Zone of Truth* spell.

**2. The Exploration Encounter: The False Wall of the Netherese Vault**

You are deep within a frozen, ruined tower. The map says a secret library room exists, but you are staring at a blank, solid stone wall.

**Round 1 (Activation):** You place your hand on your hilt, close your eyes, and open your divine senses to scan the dead-end corridor.

**The DM’s Description:** *“The solid masonry of the wall before you suddenly turns translucent in your mind’s eye, revealing a faint, glowing blue lattice of ancient glyphs holding an illusory script in place. Behind the stone texture, you can clearly see the dark outline of an arched wooden doorway and a dust-covered iron lever.”*

**The Mechanical Result:** You have successfully pierced a high-level *Hallucinatory Terrain* or *Illusion* spell designed to hide the library entrance. Because your ability reveals it as a falsehood, you grant yourself and your adventuring party a **+4 bonus to bypass or disbelieve the illusion**, allowing you to bypass a tedious search check and head straight into the archive.

**<u>ΕΝΑΛΛΑΚΤΙΚΑ:</u>**

Μπορούμε να τον κάνουμε και dual-class ή και multi-class, με τη λογική ότι έφθασε π.χ 7 level fighter και στη συνέχεια «γύρισε» σε Ιερέα. Το ίδιο μπορούμε και αν ήταν π.χ Fighter/Thief. Και σε αυτή την περίπτωση μπορούμε να «παγώσουμε» τα 2 classes.

Οι αλλαγές εδώ θα είναι οι εξής:

Race: Human

Alignment: True Neutral, Lawful Neutral, or Neutral Good.

Attribute Minimums: Strength 15+ and Wisdom 17+.

Recommended Intelligence: 14+.

**Fighter Phase:** You must spend proficiency slots to gain **Weapon Specialization (Longsword)**.

**Cleric Phase Restriction:** Oghma is an exception to the "blunt weapons only" cleric rule. His specialty priests and devout dual/multi-class champions are **explicitly allowed to use the Longsword** because it is Oghma's favored holy weapon.

Non-Weapon Proficiencies

**Bonus (Free):** Reading/Writing (Native), Ancient History.

**Academic Pool (Requires Intelligence):** Religion, Ancient Languages, Local History, Spellcraft.

Special Benefits

**The Scholar's Specialization:** Once your Cleric level surpasses your Fighter level, you regain your Fighter weapon specialization. You may attack with your longsword utilizing your Fighter THAC0 and damage bonuses, while simultaneously casting Cleric spells.

**Legend Lore (As a Bard):** Because you are integrated into *The Children of the Passive Voice*, you have access to forbidden indexes. You gain a base **5% chance per Cleric level** to identify the history, properties, or curses of any ancient text, magical tome, or historical relic you uncover.

**Oghma's Mental Shield:** Your devotion to objective truth grants you a **+2 bonus on saving throws against mental illusions**, *Charm* spells, or magical memory erasure (such as the *Forget* spell).

Special Hindrances

**The Vow of Truth:** You cannot utter a deliberate lie. If a truth is too dangerous to speak, you must remain silent or rely on evasion. Destroying a book or allowing a library to burn intentionally strips you of your Cleric spell-casting abilities until you perform an arduous task of archival recovery.

**The Tithe of the Scriptorium:** You are forbidden from hoarding personal wealth. All gold, gems, and treasure gained from adventuring—beyond what is strictly required to feed yourself and repair your armor—must be signed over to *The Children of the Passive Voice* to fund the copying of manuscripts and the building of regional libraries.

**The Academic Encumbrance:** You must travel with heavy writing desks, inks, blank scrolls, and massive reference books. This imposes a permanent **15 lbs of un-droppable encumbrance** to your inventory, slightly restricting how much extra dungeon loot you can personally carry out.
