**<u>ALLAUNDO PROPHECY OF MALYGRIS</u>**

Αυτή είναι μια από τις πολλές προφητείες του Alaundo, που ο Khelben θεωρεί ότι αναφέρεται στα γεγονότα του Blood Moon, την Redhanded και το Dracolich!

**The Prophecy of the Bone Gale:** *Όπως καταγράφηκε στο 92nd Scroll του Allaundo, το έτος 76 DR.*

*When moon is bled and bound in chains,*
*When red flame gutters but does not die,*
*When the first crown lies shattered in dust,*
*The desert shall speak with a voice not its own.*

*Beware the dragon who sleeps without death,*
*Whose ribs are crypts and whose breath is memory.*
*He shall be clothed in veils thrice-folded:*
*Of shadow, of blood, of forgotten oath.*

*A hand unclean, yet veiled by mercy’s daughter,*
*Shall sew into his bones a spark not hers,*
*A god’s wound stolen, hidden from its sire,*
*Set to beat within a heart of sand.*

*Then shall the Balance falter, yet not break,*
*For laws older than moonrise shield the deed.*
*The gods will see but not strike,*
*The wise will know but dare not speak,*
*And the realm shall shiver at a silence louder than war.*

*Mark the Black Dunes, O children of dawn—*
*Mark the place where oaths are broken and shadows feast.*
*For from there shall rise the Bone Gale, the Ebon Wyrm,*
*Neither god nor beast, but a wound that flies.*

*And only the hand that suffers willingly,*
*And only the blade that bleeds for another,*
*May turn aside the storm.*

**Σχόλια και ερμηνείες:**

**“When moon is bled”** → The Blood Moon phenomenon of 1160 DR.

**“First crown shattered in dust”** → The fall of Netheril and Xinlenal, first enclave.

**“Dragon who sleeps without death”** → Malygris, dracolich awaiting release.

**“Hand unclean… veiled by mercy’s daughter”** → Redhanded (born of Glasya → “mercy’s daughter” twisted).

**“A god’s wound stolen”** → The shard of Bhaal’s essence.

**“Balance falter, yet not break”** → The Elder Compact loophole shielding Malygris.
