# Level-1 Spell Evaluation — for Selanar Durothil

Two-axis rating of every level-1 wizard spell in the RAW mirror. Fable pass, 2026-07-25.

## The frame (what's actually being decided)

- **Six spells** go in the spellbook at wizard level 1; **four** are prepared. +2 book spells every level thereafter; prepared count grows per the class table.
- **Ritual Adept changes the math:** any ritual-tagged spell casts *from the book without being prepared* (10 extra minutes). Rituals cost a book slot but **zero prepared slots** — their effective price is halved. Detect Magic, Find Familiar, Identify, Comprehend Languages, Alarm, Unseen Servant all benefit.
- **Memorize Spell (L5)** later allows one prepared swap per short rest — niche picks gain value once the book is deep.
- Levers carried forward: radiant feeds **Spellfire Adept** (planned L12); attack rolls feed **Elven Accuracy** (planned L4); Blinded/Prone conditions feed his own melee advantage → Elven Accuracy; concentration belongs to the signature blade (Iridescent Blade at L3+); the "long rests require safe locations" house rule inflates warding rituals; the Nether Scrolls quest inflates ancient-text utility.

## Recommended opening book (6), prepared (4), and the L2 additions

**⚠ Name-collision warning, verified against the JSON:** the Spellfire Spark feat's cantrip benefit is titled *"Spellfire **Flame**"* (it grants **Sacred Flame**); *"Spellfire **Flare**"* is an unrelated level-1 spell that nothing in this build grants — it must be scribed like any wizard spell. Don't let the names fool you twice. **Detect Magic**, by contrast, genuinely is always-prepared from the High Elf lineage at character level 3 — so it's cut from the opening book. Nuance: the lineage version isn't a *book copy*, so Ritual Adept can't ritual-cast it; scribe it into the book later (50 gp, downtime) if unlimited slot-free castings prove worth it.

**Book at L1:** Shield · Mage Armor · Absorb Elements · Spellfire Flare · **Color Spray** · **Find Familiar** (ritual)
— Color Spray takes Detect Magic's vacated slot: combat variety plus the prismatic identity from day one.

**Prepared (4):** Shield · Mage Armor · Absorb Elements · Spellfire Flare.
**Swap pattern:** Color Spray in for Absorb Elements on days promising crowds rather than elements (post-L5, Memorize Spell makes this a short-rest decision, not a dawn one).

**L2 additions (+2):** **Comprehend Languages** (ritual — the Nether Scrolls quest in spell form) · **Alarm** (ritual — the "long rests need a safe location" house rule, weaponized). Both cost zero prepared slots. Prepared grows to 5 at L2 → Color Spray joins the prepared four.

**Book growth priorities (L3–4):** Identify (ritual) → Detect Magic (book copy, for the ritual casting) → Protection from Evil and Good / Disguise Self / Feather Fall as the campaign dictates. Rituals stack in the book like tools in a kit — never dead weight.

**For the Crusty bundle (add):** faerie-dragon cosmetic reskin for Find Familiar's form; a "Spellfire Bolt" reskin for Fire Bolt remains optional now that Spellfire Flare exists in print.
