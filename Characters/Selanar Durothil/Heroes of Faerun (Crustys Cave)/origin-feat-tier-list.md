## Rating scales

**Narrative** — fit with the established character (elven noble mage of House Durothil; Myth Drannor/Akh'Faer; Corellon-initiated Faerna; Silver Twilight scholar & Seeker of Mysteries; **Harper**; bon viveur with a **faerie dragon familiar** and the motto "Blood, lust, and pixie dust") **and his declared aesthetic** — an **iridescent / prismatic / radiant-light** theme with an explicit **spellfire** motif (per the reskin doc: Shadow Blade→*Iridescent Blade*, Green-Flame Blade→*Spellfire Blade*, Booming Blade→*Echoing Light Blade*):
★☆☆☆☆ contradicts him · ★★☆☆☆ neutral/stretch · ★★★☆☆ flavorable · ★★★★☆ strong resonance · ★★★★★ already written into his story / perfect thematic match.

## Levers that move these ratings for *this* character (not a generic list)

1. **Dynamic initiative** (reroll every round) — inflates every initiative effect.
2. **Lethal, no-armor d6 caster** — raw survivability and damage-mitigation gain value.
3. **"Cool > effective"** DM ethos — narrative fit is a legitimate deciding factor, not a tiebreaker.
4. **Investigation/mystery-heavy** — Search/Study/knowledge utility punches above its weight.
5. **Party has a Knowledge Cleric** (Vesper) — healing + Guidance already covered; don't pick a feat *to fill that gap*.
6. **Bladesong = INT monostat** — weapon attack/damage, AC, Concentration, and uses all key off INT; no origin feat scales with INT, so feat value here is utility/survivability/theme, never stat-multiplication.
7. **Light/prismatic/spellfire aesthetic** — radiant/light/fey-flavored options now score high on narrative where "shadow" or off-theme options score low.

## Verdict

Reading the reskin doc reshuffles the top meaningfully from the last pass:

- **New standout for *this* character — Spellfire Spark (FRHoF).** It's the only *available* feat that scores ★★★★★ narrative while still doing real work: it directly instantiates his declared **spellfire** motif and his **radiant/light** damage theme (Sacred Flame), adds passive damage mitigation a squishy caster wants in a lethal game, and slots cleanly into his action economy (Action: melee cantrip; Bonus: Sacred Flame). It dominates Harper Agent — matching it on theme while vastly outdoing it mechanically.
- **Optimizer's picks — Alert or Lucky** (both ★★★★★ mechanical). Alert is the table-inflated pick (initiative every round); Lucky is the raw-survival pick and now flavors passably via the "pixie dust" fey-luck angle.
- **Ask the DM about off-setting content before finalizing.** If Crusty allows non-FR books, **Child of the Sun** (★★★/★★★★★ — Faerie Fire is *the* prismatic-light feat) and **Sharp Eye** (★★★★/★★★ — the investigation-game sleeper) are both stronger fits than most of the FR-legal list. This is the single highest-leverage question to resolve, and it pairs naturally with getting the spell-reskin approval you already owe him.

**My lead is now Spellfire Spark**, with Alert as the pure-power alternative — and a flag to ask Crusty about (a) the three spell reskins and (b) whether off-setting feats like Child of the Sun are on the table, since one of his best thematic options lives there.

Standing correction from the mechanical re-read: the +1 DEX (Mythalkeeper) is right for AC/initiative/DEX-saves, **not** weapon attacks — 2024 Bladesong routes weapon attack/damage through INT.
