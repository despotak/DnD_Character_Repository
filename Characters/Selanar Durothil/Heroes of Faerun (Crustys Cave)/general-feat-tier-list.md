# General Feat Evaluation — for Selanar Durothil

Two-axis rating (same scales as `origin-feat-tier-list.md`) of every feat available to the level 4/8/12/16 slots. Fable pass, 2026-07-25. Epic Boons (level 19) are a separate category, deferred.

## Scope

The RAW mirror holds **80 General-category feats** (XPHB 43, EFA 14, FRHoF 13, ABH 10) plus the 2014-era feats (category-less; PHB/XGE/TCE/FTD are table-legal under "all 2014/2024 content permitted"). Also relevant: **2024 rules allow Origin feats in level-4+ slots** — so Alert, Lucky, Tough, etc. from the origin list remain takeable (see `origin-feat-tier-list.md` for their ratings; they carry over unchanged).

**Excluded, by name:**
- **EFA (14):** Eberron dragonmark/house feats — off-setting.
- **FRHoF chain-locked (7):** Dragonscarred, Enclave Magic, Harper Teamwork, Lordly Resolve, Order's Resilience, Purple Dragon Commandant, Zhentarim Tactics — each requires an origin feat he didn't take (he took Spellfire Spark). Harper Teamwork is the one to *note*: if the feat choice ever gets rebuilt around Harper Agent, this is its payoff.
- **2014 species-locked he can't take:** Bountiful Luck, Second Chance (halfling), Dragon Fear/Hide (dragonborn), Dwarven Fortitude, Squat Nimbleness (dwarf/small), Fade Away (gnome), Flames of Phlegethos, Infernal Constitution (tiefling), Orcish Fury (half-orc), Prodigy (half-elf/half-orc/human), Drow High Magic, Wood Elf Magic (wrong lineage).
- **Bladesong-breakers (auto-D, mechanically dead):** every armor/shield feat — Lightly/Moderately/Heavily Armored, Medium/Heavy Armor Master, Shield Master. Bladesong requires no armor, no shield. Non-negotiable.
- **ABH (10):** vampire-book feats (Bloodlust, Bomber, Delicious Pain, Love Bites, Putrefy, Treacherous Allure, Vampire Touched, Cloying Mists) — mostly off-concept, **except Light Bringer and Rebuke, promoted to Group B below** — ABH is Baldur's Gate-set, so closer to table-legal than Lorwyn/Ravenloft was for origin feats; still flag for approval.

## Levers specific to this pass (beyond the origin-list levers, which all still apply)

1. **NEW HOUSE RULE — any feat's ASI can route to any ability score.** The printed "+1 to X, Y, or Z" list on a feat no longer constrains where its point goes. This fully decouples feat *selection* (score on features alone — reroll mechanics, reactions, damage, utility) from ASI *routing* (score on breakpoint math, below). Every "routes dead" / "wasted ASI" note elsewhere in this doc predates the house rule and is now obsolete — any feat, regardless of its printed pool, can deliver a live point to whichever stat needs it.
2. **INT is already 20 at level 1** (rolled array + background +2) — capped at the default ability-score max, so any point routed to INT is dead regardless of the house rule. Never route a feat's ASI here.
3. **DEX (16) and CON (14) both sit even — first +1 is "setup," second completes the breakpoint. WIS (8) is a dead lane. CHA (13) is the only odd stat.** Post-swap array (2026-07-28: the rolled 13/8 traded between WIS and CHA to match the character concept) → INT 20 / DEX 16 / CON 14 / WIS 8 / CHA 13 / STR 7. A single +1 to DEX or CON doesn't move the modifier; a second +1 does (DEX 16→18: +3→+4; CON 14→16: +2→+3). WIS 8: one point does nothing (8→9), two only reach +0 — never route a feat's point here. CHA 13: a single +1 breakpoints to 14 (+1→+2) — mechanically minor (unproficient social, CHA saves), so it's the designated target for *bonus* ASI sources (Epic Boon, table-wide L19 boon, magic items), not feat slots. Locked plan (build-progression.md § 6): **L4+L8 → the DEX pair, L12+L16 → the CON pair.** STR (7) breakpoints at +1 too but stays the dump.
4. **The radiant reskin engine (pending approval):** Iridescent Blade / Echoing Light Blade / Spellfire Blade convert his weapon-magic entirely to radiant (Spellfire Blade updated fire→radiant to match published spellfire effects). Several feats below (Spellfire Adept, Rebuke, Light Bringer) key off radiant damage or bright light — **their value is partly contingent on the reskins being approved.** RAW-only fallback: Sacred Flame and True Strike are already radiant.
5. **The visible-casting house rule** ("Verbal are loud, can not be whispered, Somatic are flashy") — **Subtle Spell ignores exactly this.** Metamagic Adept is table-inflated in an intrigue campaign the way Alert is initiative-inflated.
6. **Reaction economy is contested from L10:** Song of Defense, Shield (spell), Defensive Duelist, War Caster's Reactive Spell, Mythal Touched's ward all want the same reaction. Don't stack more than ~two reaction options.

## Verdict — and a suggested slot sequence

The headline discovery: **his feats can be an identity arc, not a shopping list.** Spellfire Spark (origin) → **Spellfire Adept** → the radiant reskin package → **Light Bringer/Rebuke** form an actual *build-around* — the prismatic-light theme with teeth. Its ceiling depends on Crusty approving the reskins, which makes the pitch bundle even more load-bearing.

Suggested sequence under the rolled array + the any-feat-any-stat house rule (each slot re-decided when reached, per the one-step-at-a-time process):

- **L4 — Elven Accuracy** (+1 DEX→17, setup — see lever 3): the offense engine comes online with Iridescent Blade already castable; the reroll mechanic carries the pick, the DEX pair completes next slot.
- **L8 — War Caster** (+1 DEX→18, breakpoint — houserule reroute off its printed INT/WIS/CON list): concentration becomes near-unbreakable right as concentration spells define his tier, *and* AC/initiative permanently tick up from here.
- **L12 — Spellfire Adept** (+1 CON→15, setup): the identity capstone, taken once the radiant engine is proven at the table; its point opens the CON pair.
- **L16 — Fey-Touched (Gift of Alacrity)** or **Resilient** or **Keen Mind/Observant** (expertise) or **Alert** (+1 CON→16, breakpoint — whichever feat wins the slot, its point completes the pair: +16 retroactive HP, +1 Concentration), depending on what the campaign has been punishing. Fey-Touched enters as frontrunner if both its DM gates clear (EGW legality + the item-(d) initiative-reroll ruling): party-wide +1d8 initiative every round, all day, off cheap L1 slots.
- **L19 — Epic Boon**: separate pass, plus clarify how it stacks with the table-wide level-19 boon house rule; if it grants an ASI, CHA 13→14 is the last cheap single-point breakpoint on the board.

Ratings reflect the rolled array with the 2026-07-28 WIS/CHA swap (7,8,13,14,15,18 → INT 20/DEX 16/CON 14/WIS 8/CHA 13/STR 7 post-background) and the any-feat-any-stat house rule — both now locked in, nothing left pending here.
