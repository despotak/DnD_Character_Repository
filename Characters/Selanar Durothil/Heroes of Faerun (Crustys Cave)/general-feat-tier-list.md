# General Feat Evaluation — for Selanar Durothil

Two-axis rating (same scales as `origin-feat-tier-list.md`) of every feat available to the level 4/8/12/16 slots. Fable pass, 2026-07-25. Epic Boons (level 19) are a separate category, deferred.

## Scope

The RAW mirror holds **80 General-category feats** (XPHB 43, EFA 14, FRHoF 13, ABH 10) plus the 2014-era feats (category-less; PHB/XGE/TCE/FTD are table-legal under "all 2014/2024 content permitted"). Also relevant: **2024 rules allow Origin feats in level-4+ slots** — so Alert, Lucky, Tough, etc. from the origin list remain takeable (see `origin-feat-tier-list.md` for their ratings; they carry over unchanged).

**Excluded, by name:**
- **EFA (14):** Eberron dragonmark/house feats — off-setting.
- **FRHoF chain-locked (7):** Dragonscarred, Enclave Magic, Harper Teamwork, Lordly Resolve, Order's Resilience, Purple Dragon Commandant, Zhentarim Tactics — each requires an origin feat he didn't take (he took Spellfire Spark). Harper Teamwork is the one to *note*: if the feat choice ever gets rebuilt around Harper Agent, this is its payoff.
- **2014 species-locked he can't take:** Bountiful Luck, Second Chance (halfling), Dragon Fear/Hide (dragonborn), Dwarven Fortitude, Squat Nimbleness (dwarf/small), Fade Away (gnome), Flames of Phlegethos, Infernal Constitution (tiefling), Orcish Fury (half-orc), Prodigy (half-elf/half-orc/human), Drow High Magic, Wood Elf Magic (wrong lineage).
- **Bladesong-breakers (auto-D, mechanically dead):** every armor/shield feat — Lightly/Moderately/Heavily Armored, Medium/Heavy Armor Master, Shield Master. Bladesong requires no armor, no shield. Non-negotiable.
- **ABH (10):** vampire-book feats (Bloodlust, Bomber, Delicious Pain, Love Bites, Putrefy, Treacherous Allure, Vampire Touched, Cloying Mists) — mostly off-concept, **except Light Bringer and Rebuke, promoted to Group B below** — ABH is Baldur's Gate-set, so closer to table-legal than Lorwyn/Ravenloft was for origin feats; still flag for approval.

## Levers specific to this pass (beyond the origin-list levers, which all still apply)

1. **INT is already 20 at level 1** (assumed array + background +2) — "+1 INT" riders are dead weight unless the real Monday roll comes in lower. Feats offering INT/WIS/CHA route their +1 to WIS. *Contingency: if the real array leaves INT at 19, every +1-INT feat gains a half-step.*
2. **DEX 17 is odd** — any +1-DEX feat crosses to 18 (+4): AC 19 under Bladesong, better initiative every round. Real breakpoint.
3. **The radiant reskin engine (pending approval):** Iridescent Blade / Echoing Light Blade / Spellfire Blade convert his weapon-magic entirely to radiant (Spellfire Blade updated fire→radiant to match published spellfire effects). Several feats below (Spellfire Adept, Rebuke, Light Bringer) key off radiant damage or bright light — **their value is partly contingent on the reskins being approved.** RAW-only fallback: Sacred Flame and True Strike are already radiant.
4. **The visible-casting house rule** ("Verbal are loud, can not be whispered, Somatic are flashy") — **Subtle Spell ignores exactly this.** Metamagic Adept is table-inflated in an intrigue campaign the way Alert is initiative-inflated.
5. **Reaction economy is contested from L10:** Song of Defense, Shield (spell), Defensive Duelist, War Caster's Reactive Spell, Mythal Touched's ward all want the same reaction. Don't stack more than ~two reaction options.

## Verdict — and a suggested slot sequence

The headline discovery: **his feats can be an identity arc, not a shopping list.** Spellfire Spark (origin) → **Spellfire Adept** → the radiant reskin package → **Light Bringer/Rebuke** form an actual *build-around* — the prismatic-light theme with teeth. Its ceiling depends on Crusty approving the reskins, which makes the pitch bundle even more load-bearing.

Suggested sequence under the assumed array (each slot re-decided when reached, per the one-step-at-a-time process):

- **L4 — Elven Accuracy** (+1 DEX→18): the offense engine comes online with Iridescent Blade already castable.
- **L8 — War Caster** (+1 WIS): concentration becomes near-unbreakable right as concentration spells define his tier.
- **L12 — Spellfire Adept** (+1 WIS→15): the identity capstone, taken once the radiant engine is proven at the table.
- **L16 — Resilient (CON→16)** or **Keen Mind/Observant** (expertise) or **Alert**, depending on what the campaign has been punishing.
- **L19 — Epic Boon**: separate pass, plus clarify how it stacks with the table-wide level-19 boon house rule.

All ratings assume the placeholder array — re-run lever 1 and 2 when the real stats land Monday.
