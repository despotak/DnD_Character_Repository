# Level-2 Spell Evaluation — for Selanar Durothil

Two-axis rating of every level-2 Wizard-list spell in the RAW mirror (5etools, pulled 2026-09-08): 68 spells, after dropping the 2014 entries that the 2024 book reprinted. Same scales as `spell-tier-list-L1.md`. Level 3 is in `spell-tier-list-L3.md`. Nyx pass, 2026-11-09.

## Where the book stands (check this first)

From his Foundry sheet on 2026-11-09, Selanar is a level-2 wizard with eight spells in the book:

- **Opening six:** Shield · Mage Armor · Spellfire Flare · Find Familiar (ritual) · Detect Magic (ritual) · Sleep
- **Copied at level 2:** Absorb Elements · Silvery Barbs (Journal Entry VIII)
- **Prepared (5):** Mage Armor · Shield · Silvery Barbs · Sleep · Spellfire Flare
- **Cantrips:** True Strike · Mind Sliver · Mage Hand · Prestidigitation (lineage slot) · Spellfire Gleam (the feat's Sacred Flame: 60 ft, DEX save)

That isn't the plan in `build-progression.md` § 8, which had Absorb Elements and Color Spray in the opening six and Comprehend Languages and Alarm at level 2. **Color Spray, Comprehend Languages, Alarm, Booming Blade and Light aren't on the sheet.** Silvery Barbs, penciled for L3, is already in, so both L3 picks are free.

## The frame

- **Slots, not just book space.** Level-2 slots arrive at wizard L3: two at L3, three from L4. Each level adds two book spells of any level he has slots for, so the free level-2 picks are **L3 and L4, four in all**. From L5 they compete with level-3 spells for the same two per level. Anything else is copied from a found scroll or book: **2 hours and 100 GP** (50 GP per spell level).
- **The bar a level-2 slot must clear is his own Spellfire Flare, upcast.** Two blasts of 2d10 radiant (22 if both hit), separate attack rolls, cover ignored. A one-shot level-2 spell that does less than that wastes the slot. The bar has one weakness: Flare is a ranged spell attack, so in melee it rolls at Disadvantage. Spells that work at sword's length gain from that.
- **Concentration belongs to the blade.** The Iridescent Blade (Shadow Blade chassis) holds concentration for a minute. Every other concentration spell on this list replaces the blade for that fight rather than adding to it. Spells with no concentration stack with it, and are rated up here for that reason.
- **The reaction is full:** Shield, Absorb Elements, Silvery Barbs. **So is the bonus action:** Bladesong, Sacred Flame (Proficiency-times per day), and the blade's cast and recall. Bonus-action spells pay a tax unless he casts them before the fight.
- **Lineage:** Misty Step arrives always prepared at character L5 (free once per long rest, or cast with a slot). Never scribe it, the same verdict as Detect Magic in the L1 list.
- **Spell Mastery (L18)** makes one L1 and one L2 spell from the book castable at will, but only spells "that have a casting time of an action". **The blade, Misty Step and Magic Weapon are bonus actions and can't be chosen.** Mirror Image can. (Shield is a reaction, so it can't be the L1 pick either.)
- **Table levers:** Dirty Critical (beat AC by 10 for a crit, both ways) makes every Advantage source and the Paralyzed condition worth more, and makes anything that turns hits into misses worth more on defense. Elven Accuracy (L4) triples every INT attack with Advantage. Radiant spell damage feeds Spellfire Adept (L12), whose trigger is *"when a spell you cast deals Radiant damage"*. Visible casting: verbal can't be whispered and somatic can't be hidden, so spells with no verbal component are the quiet ones. Material components cost gold; prices are noted per spell, and the sword covers every uncosted component as his focus. Monster resistances are heavily modified, so force damage and magical weapons matter.
- **Party overlap** (the roster Christos gave on 2026-11-10 is in `campaign-info.md`):
  - **Faelyn is a second wizard,** so every spell on this list is on Faelyn's list too, and none of them is Selanar's alone. Each spellbook is the other's best scroll: copying from Faelyn's book costs the same 2 hours and 50 GP per spell level as copying a found scroll, so his free picks should go to spells Faelyn hasn't taken. Faelyn can also cast a buff *on* him, which costs none of his slots and, for a concentration buff, none of his concentration.
  - **Faelyn's book will lean to Enchantment.** The *Arcana Unleashed* Enchanter (the only subclass by that name) adds two Enchantment spells of level 2 or lower to the book free at level 3, and one more with each new spell level. The 2014 School of Enchantment halves the cost of copying them instead. Either way, expect Hold Person and Suggestion there early; Tasha's Mind Whip and Crown of Madness are the other level-2 Enchantments. Which Enchanter Faelyn runs isn't recorded.
  - **Vesper** is a Knowledge cleric, but the build files don't record which Knowledge Domain. The 2024 one (FRHoF) gives him **Detect Thoughts and Mind Spike** always prepared at cleric 3. The 2014 one (PHB) gives **Augury and Suggestion**. One of those pairs reaches the table free either way, and those spells are rated down for it; worth asking Kale. His cleric list also carries Hold Person, Blindness/Deafness and Continual Flame.
  - **Cad** is a ranger (Hollow Warden), a half caster with level-2 slots from ranger 5. His list shares Magic Weapon, Enhance Ability and Locate Object with this one, and the Hollow Warden has Alter Self always prepared from ranger 5. **Robbie** (Thief) casts nothing.

## The snag in the centerpiece: the blade has no price

True Strike's material component is *"a weapon with which you have proficiency and that is worth 1+ CP"*. Booming Blade's and Green-Flame Blade's is *"a melee weapon worth at least 1 sp"*. Shadow Blade *"counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient"*, but it has no price, so **RAW the blade can't be the weapon for True Strike, or for Booming Blade and Green-Flame Blade (Echoing Light Blade and Spellfire Blade, reskinned), which join his daily cantrip rotation at L3.**

Before L6 this costs almost nothing, because swinging the blade with the Attack action (2d8+5, Advantage in light) already matches or beats any of the three through steel (1d8+5 plus the cantrip's rider, and a die more from L5). From L6, Extra Attack lets one of the two attacks become a cantrip, and that cantrip then has to go through a real sword in his other hand, or not happen. One line added to the reskin bundle fixes it (question (l) below).

## C/D — surveyed, dismissed

**Darkness** (it dispels his own light spells of 2nd level or lower and kills the reskin's Advantage: the anti-spell for this character), **Arcane Vigor** (spends the Hit Dice Spellfire Adept will want), **Snilloc's Snowball Swarm / Rime's Binding Ice** (cold, anti-theme), **Aganazzar's Scorcher / Flaming Sphere / Dragon's Breath** (fire, and the last two eat bonus actions), **Shatter** (thunder, loud, 13.5 a target), **Melf's Acid Arrow, Cloud of Daggers, Crown of Madness, Ray of Enfeeblement, Wither and Bloom, Uncertain Footing**, **Alter Self** (Disguise Self covers it, and Cad has it always prepared from ranger 5), **Darkvision** (he has it), **Spider Climb, Gust of Wind, Warding Wind, Dust Devil, Earthbind, Maximilian's Earthen Grasp, Enlarge/Reduce, Nathair's Mischief, Air Bubble, Skywrite, Warp Sense, Flock of Familiars**, **Misty Step** (lineage; never scribe), **Gift of Gab / Jim's Glowing Coin** (AI, off-register, the same verdict as the L1 list).

## Recommended: L3 and L4 book additions

**L3 (+2):** **Iridescent Blade** · **Mirror Image**.
**Prepared at L3 (6):** Shield · Mage Armor · Spellfire Flare · Silvery Barbs · Iridescent Blade · Mirror Image. Sleep or Absorb Elements swaps in for Mirror Image when the day promises crowds or elemental damage. Detect Magic becomes always prepared from the lineage, on top of the six, and Find Familiar stays a ritual.

**L4 (+2):** **Blindness/Deafness** · **Detect Thoughts**. If Vesper's domain already gives him Detect Thoughts, take **Battle Familiar** in its place (once (h) clears), or whatever level-2 spell Faelyn's book lacks. Hold Person is a copy from Faelyn's book, not a pick.

**Ask the party, not the book:** Continual Flame (Vesper or Faelyn, cast once) and Magic Weapon on his longsword (Faelyn, or Cad from ranger 5).

**Copy from Faelyn's book or a found scroll (100 GP each):** Magic Weapon → Hold Person → Battle Familiar → Rope Trick (if Faelyn's book doesn't have it) → Borrowed Knowledge. Rituals (Gentle Repose, Dueling Ground) whenever convenient; they never take a prepared slot.

This replaces two earlier pencils: the build doc's "Silvery Barbs at L3" (already in the book from L2) and the AU list's "Battle Familiar at L3" (moved to L4 or a scroll).

**L18 Spell Mastery, level-2 half:** Mirror Image.
