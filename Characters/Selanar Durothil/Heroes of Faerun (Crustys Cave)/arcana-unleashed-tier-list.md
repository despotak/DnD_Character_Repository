# Arcana Unleashed — rated for Selanar Durothil

Every feat, spell and magic item in *Arcana Unleashed* (AU, released 2026-09-15), rated on the same two axes as `origin-feat-tier-list.md` and `general-feat-tier-list.md`: **Mech** is value to *this* build at *this* table, **Narr** is fit with the established character and the prismatic-light / spellfire identity. Read from the 5etools mirror (v2.35.1). Page numbers are AU pages.

## Scope

- **Feats (29):** 15 General, 10 Origin, 1 Fighting Style, 3 Epic Boons. Locked out: **Magic Connoisseur** (needs Magic Initiate, which he doesn't have) and **Arcane Warrior** (needs the Fighting Style feature).
- **Spells (33):** 30 are on the Wizard list and rated. **Summon Dinosaur** and **Summon Plant** (druid/ranger) and **Zone of Amicability** (bard/warlock) are off-list. Zone of Amicability is still reachable through Boon of Magic School Mastery (see Epic Boons).
- **Magic items:** every item, the evolving items, and the evolution rules in ch. 5. Not rated: the ten faction trinket tables (mundane), ammunition (he has no ranged weapon), and armor (it breaks Bladesong).
- **Not rated because not asked:** the four revised Wizard subclasses (Conjurer, Enchanter, Necromancer, Transmuter; none touch Bladesinger), the eight Arcane Shot options, backgrounds, factions, patrons.
- **Legality:** AU falls under "all 2014/2024 content permitted", but the ruling predates the book, so a one-line confirmation is cheap.

## Levers this book pulls

1. **Any feat's ASI routes to any stat (house rule).** Every AU General feat prints INT/WIS/CHA, and none of that matters: its +1 still lands on the DEX pair (L4/L8) or the CON pair (L12/L16). **Origin feats taken in a General slot carry no ASI at all**, so each one costs a full breakpoint step. That cost is priced into every origin rating below.
2. **The INT cap is 20 for feats, but 30 for Epic Boons.** At L19, INT is a live stat again. Boon of Erupting Spellpower's +2 takes it to 22 (+6), which is +1 to Bladesong AC, weapon attack and damage, spell DC and Concentration.
3. **Visible casting (house rule).** Verbal is loud and somatic is flashy, so anything that strips components (Enchantment Adept, Illusion Adept, Spell Duelist's Trophy) is worth more at this table than on paper.
4. **Heavily modified monster resistances (house rule).** Knowing and dodging resistances is live information here, which lifts Arcanist's Bestiary, Prismatic Rune and Spellfire Adept's resistance piercing.
5. **Initiative is rerolled every round.** "Until you roll Initiative" recharges, and anything that grants initiative advantage, pay out every round. This is the same ruling as open question (d).
6. **The reaction is already spoken for** (Shield, Absorb Elements, Silvery Barbs, War Caster, and Song of Defense from L10). **So is the bonus action** (Bladesong, Sacred Flame, Song of Victory from L14). Options that want either pay a tax.
7. **Attunement slots = Proficiency Bonus** (2 → 6 across the campaign).
8. **AU contains zero radiant spells.** Nothing in the book feeds the radiant engine directly. Elemental Familiar and Prismatic Rune both list five damage types and leave radiant off.

## Magic items

### The real find: *Evolving Magic Items* (ch. 5)

★★★★★ Mech · ★★★★★ Narr. Not an item but a DM framework, and the one part of the book that changes his long arc.

- **Rules:** an item gains rarity at tier breaks (the DM decides when), **keeps every earlier property**, adds one new property per step, and tops out at Legendary. **Crafting Evolution** lets a character do it in downtime: Uncommon → Rare costs **40 days and 1,800 gp**. The DMG crafting rules (XDMG p.220) require Arcana *and* proficiency with the item category's tool. He has the first; for a sword he lacks the second, so a smith assistant or a downtime tool proficiency closes the gap.
- **Eligible** for *complex* evolution: any **Armor, Staff or Weapon that requires Attunement**, isn't cursed, and is Very Rare or lower. A plain +1 weapon gets *simple* evolution (+1 → +2 → +3) instead.
- **His wishlist already contains eligible items.** The **Longsword of Warning** (Uncommon pick) requires attunement, and so does the **Sun Blade** (Rare pick). The Moon-Touched Longsword does not, and the Arcane Grimoire isn't a staff, armor or weapon.
- **Proposed ladder for the Longsword of Warning.** Weapon of Warning *already* gives him and allies within 30 ft Advantage on Initiative (XDMG p.324), which under per-round rerolls is **party-wide advantage every round**. So **skip Vigilant**, which would duplicate it:
  - **Rare:** *Studious* (+1d6 on a failed Study check, 3/day; investigation) or *Quickening* (bonus action: double speed and Disadvantage on opportunity attacks against him for 1 minute).
  - **Very Rare:** *Fortunate* (reroll a failed D20 Test, 2/day) or *Resistant* (Psychic).
  - **Legendary:** ***Prismatic*** (Prismatic Spray, DC 18, daily). By the book's own naming rule it becomes a **"Prismatic Longsword of Warning"**. *Heliacal* (Sunburst) is the Sun Blade's natural end instead.
- **Pitch value:** the chapter says outright that a DM can "structure a campaign arc around one of these items… similar to how you might use an Artifact". That is published scaffolding for the Ary'Faern'Kerym vestige arc (`artblade-item-card.md`), not a homebrew ask.
