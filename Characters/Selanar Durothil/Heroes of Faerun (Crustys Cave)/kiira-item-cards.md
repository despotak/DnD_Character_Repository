# The Kiira of the Tel'Quessir — 5e Item Cards
### Three-card set: the lore-gems of the elven Houses, translated for D&D 5e (2024)

> **Design contract.** No kiira has ever been printed in 5e — these translations work from the prior-edition sources directly: *Cormanthyr: Empire of Elves* (2e, p. 152–153), *Lost Empires of Faerûn* (3e, p. 155–156, the priced `telkiira` item), and the *Magic of Gemstones — Kiira of the Tel'Quessir* compendium. Every power below is a prior-edition ability mapped through the nearest official 5e analogue; save-or-die and permanent-drain effects are softened to 5e norms with the flavor kept. The third card follows the **Vestige of Divergence** framework (*Explorer's Guide to Wildemount*: Dormant → Awakened → Exalted) — the official 5e pattern for heirlooms that grow with a character, with every state transition a **DM-triggered story beat**.

---

## Card I — Tel'Kiira, Lore-Gem of a Noble House

*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement by an elf or half-elf with Intelligence 15 or higher)*

A polished, faceless gem no larger than the nail of the smallest finger, its color deepened by every mind it has known — the eldest run nearly jet-black. When attuned, it affixes painlessly to the bearer's forehead, and may be rendered **invisible at the bearer's will** (it is pretentious to wear it openly at all times).

- **Ward of the Unworthy.** A creature that dons a tel'kiira without meeting the attunement requirements takes **3d6 psychic damage** at the start of each of its turns until removed, and gains no benefit. *(2e: repeated feeblemind assault; 3e: negative levels — softened, flavor intact.)*
- **Loregem.** The gem is a **spellbook of up to 200 pages**. Scribing a spell into the kiira costs **no gold for materials**; copying it out onto paper costs normally. Research, translation, and lore-work channeled through the gem take **half the usual time**. *(3e verbatim; 2e half-study-time.)*
- **Memory Archive.** The gem holds **ten memories of up to an hour each** in perfect clarity. The bearer can record one as an action (regardless of length) and relive any at will, experiencing it as the one who recorded it. *(3e verbatim.)*
- **The House Remembers.** The bearer has **advantage on Intelligence (History) checks** concerning the gem's House, its allies and enemies, and the elven realms it has known — and may consult the ancestors' sense of things: once per long rest, after failing an Intelligence-based check, add **1d4** as a memory-flash guides them.
- **Clan-Warded Mind.** The bearer's thoughts **cannot be read** by spells or magical effects unless the bearer allows it. Members of the gem's founding House bypass this ward. *(2e constant clan-scoped mind blank, scoped to thought-reading.)*
- **The Lock.** Any acknowledged member of the owning House can **lock or unlock** the gem with a touch and a word (no action). A locked tel'kiira grants only its Loregem function. A wearer outside the House may attempt a DC 20 Charisma (Arcana) check to force a locked gem open; on a failure, they cannot retry until they gain a level. *(3e verbatim, DC preserved.)*

*Only eleven Houses still acknowledge possessing one: Alastrarra, Aunglor, **Durothil**, Haevault, Iliathor, Neirdre, Nimesin, Orbryn, Raedrimn, Starym, Ulondarr.*

## Card II — Selu'Kiira, High Lore Gem

*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement by an elf or half-elf spellcaster with Intelligence 15 or higher)*

The high lore gems are cut **exclusively from rainbow tourmalines** — long, faceted, sparkling crystals, larger and more ornate than any tel'kiira. They are not merely archives but **teachers**: a selu'kiira can confer a century of arcane study in a searing instant, and within the eldest sleep **the secrets of Elven High Magic itself**. A selu'kiira tells its age in color: it begins life **blue**, ages to **green**, then **black**, **brown**, **orange** — and past 3,000 years of stored knowledge it brightens to a **blistering red**, its final and most powerful state.

A selu'kiira grants everything a tel'kiira does (Card I), and:

- **The Peril of the Unworthy.** This gem defends itself in earnest. A **non-elf** who dons it takes **10d6 psychic damage** (save DC 17 CON for half) and gains nothing; an **unworthy elf or half-elf** must succeed on a DC 17 Charisma saving throw or be **possessed by the presences within** (as *magic jar*) while they carry the body somewhere safe to await a worthy bearer, after which the interloper wakes with no memory of the gem. *(2e blasted-to-ashes / possession — softened to 5e-survivable, story preserved.)* Legend holds one exception: **a vessel of Mystra's silver fire — or of raw spellfire — may touch a part of the gem's knowledge unharmed**, worthy or not. *(Magic of Gemstones, p.1.)*
- **The Weave, Understood.** The bearer casts with a comprehension few mortals reach. While attuned, the bearer gains a bonus to their **spell save DC and spell attack rolls** determined by the gem's color: **+0 (blue/green)**, **+1 (black/brown/orange)**, **+2 (red)**. *(2e save-penalty/damage riders, translated to the Rod of the Pact Keeper / Robe of the Archmagi idiom and capped.)*
- **Sight Beyond Seeming.** The bearer has **advantage on saving throws and ability checks against illusions**. *(2e "sees past illusions as two points more intelligent.")*
- **A Century in an Instant.** Once per **year**, the gem may teach: the bearer gains proficiency — or expertise, if already proficient — in one of Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion, **or** adds a number of wizard spells to the kiira-book chosen by the DM from the repertoires of past bearers. *(The 2e "novice into a mage in an instant," rationed.)*
- **The Secrets of High Magic** *(red gems only, and only for the ready)*: a bearer who is at least a **17th-level spellcaster** with **Intelligence and Wisdom 18 or higher** may be initiated into **Elven High Magic** — the qualification to learn, join, or lead the great rituals (mythal-work, realm-wards, the great songs) as **narrative events under DM adjudication**, never a slot mechanic. *(2e High Mage minimums, translated.)*

> **The dark mirror.** Twelve selu'kiira were made that no House names aloud: the **shilvael'kiira**, the Nightstar Gems of the Vyshaan — dagger-shaped, iridescent purple, each holding the bound spirit of the Selu'taar who made it and the corrupting counsel of Malkizid. They test their bearers with daily moral temptations and consume the unworthy utterly. If one ever surfaces in play, it is *not this item* — it is a villain wearing this item's face. *(Magic of Gemstones, p. 3–4 — offered as DM ammunition, no stats proposed.)*

## Card III — The Selu'Kiira of House Durothil
### *"Vyshaan'karth — the Memory of the Golden Bough"*

*Wondrous item, legendary (Vestige of Divergence) — requires attunement by an elf or half-elf of House Durothil's blood who is a spellcaster*

A long, faceted rainbow tourmaline of deep, late-summer **orange** — nearly complete. Its 3,000th year of stored knowledge falls within the current bearer's lifetime. When not worn, it rides mounted in a **platinum diadem** — the traditional setting of a gem between bearers, carried by a **caretaker** who may not wear it *(canon: the unworthy claimant serves as caretaker until a worthy brow is found)*. When attuned, it leaves the diadem and bonds to the forehead, invisible at the bearer's will.

The gem is **semi-sentient**. It does not speak; it *remembers at you* — memory-flashes, dreams, the sensation of an ancestor's hand on the shoulder. Its purpose: *preserve House Durothil, and restore the Art of the People.*

**Always active, in every state:**
- **Ward of the Unworthy** and **the silver-fire exception** — as Card II.
- **The Lock** — as Card I. *Any acknowledged member of House Durothil can silence this gem with a touch and a word. The House's matron on Evermeet has never relinquished that birthright.*

### ◆ Dormant — "the Whisper" *(from first attunement)*

First attunement demands the **Acclimation**: a three-hour communion of three successive DC 13 Intelligence saving throws; each failure inflicts one level of **exhaustion** as the flood of memory batters the mind. *(The 2e ordeal — temporary cost, no permanent drain.)*

- **Loregem** (limitless pages), **Memory Archive**, **Ancestral Insight** (1/long rest, +1d4 to a failed INT-based check), and **The House Remembers** — as Card I.

### ◆ Awakened — "the Chorus" *(DM story beat; suggested tier: levels 9–12)*

*The orange deepens toward amber; individual ancestors become distinguishable voices. Suggested trigger: an act of service to the People the ancestors judge worthy.*

- **Ancestral Spell-Gift.** On awakening, the gem surfaces **1d6 wizard spells** (of castable levels, DM-curated from the ancestors' repertoires) into the kiira-book; thereafter **one further spell per wizard level gained**. *(2e "1d6 spells per year," re-paced; DM curation is the balance valve.)*
- **Clan-Warded Mind** — as Card I, plus **advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened**.
- **Voice of Forty Generations.** Proficiency in one of Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion — or **expertise** in one already known.

### ◆ Exalted — "the Communion" *(DM story beat; suggested tier: levels 13–16)*

*Amber gives way to the first flickers of red at the heart. Suggested trigger: a deed of House-defining consequence — or reckoning over the Lock itself.*

- **Discipline of Ages.** Intelligence increases by **2, to a maximum of 22**. *(Tome of Clear Thought analogue, very rare tier.)*
- **Sight Beyond Seeming** — as Card II.
- **An Ancestor Intercedes.** Once per long rest, when the bearer fails an INT, WIS, or CHA saving throw, they **succeed instead**.

### ◆ The Blistering Red — "the Third Millennium" *(campaign-climax gate; suggested 17+)*

*During the campaign, the gem's 3,000th year completes — the item's arc ends alongside the bearer's. It brightens to blistering red.*

- **The Weave, Understood.** +1 to **spell save DC and spell attack rolls**. *(Deliberately half of Card II's red-gem cap — this gem spent the Crown Wars sealed, not learning.)*
- **Mind Blank of the Clan.** The bearer's mind is under a constant ***mind blank*** effect (clan excepted). *(2e constant effect, restored at legendary tier.)*
- **The Secrets of the Selu'Kiira.** Initiation into **Elven High Magic** — narrative capability, DM-adjudicated, exactly as in every edition's lore. *(This is the campaign goal the character has chased since level 0: Arselu'Tel'Quess restored.)*
- **Ary'Faern'Kerym resonance** *(optional, DM's discretion):* the gem knows where the Artblade sleeps. It will not say. It will *show him*, when the People need it.

## Design notes for the DM

1. **Combat footprint is nearly zero until Exalted** (a d4, some save riders); the item's weight is knowledge, identity, and hooks the DM controls — including a canonical off-switch held by an NPC antagonist. Total power at Exalted ≈ one very rare item; at Red ≈ legendary-tier but half of the generic Card II ceiling. The legendary label prices the story, not the stats.
2. **Acquisition dial:** begin play with the gem **inert and silent** in its diadem — an heirloom nobody in living memory has woken (recommended; zero level-1 power, pure fuse) — or lost in the fall of Myth Drannor, to be **retrieved** as an early arc.
3. **The silver-fire clause is a deliberate thread:** a bearer whose own spark is raw Weave-stuff may find the gem answers him *before* it should — in fragments, in dreams — which lets the item whisper at any level without granting a single mechanic.
4. **On the name:** *Vyshaan'karth* is the name the House's own records use — House Durothil styles itself "Scion of Tintageer and Aryvandaar," and the gem's earliest memories may reach back to that empire. Given what else Aryvandaar's Selu'taar made (see Card II's dark mirror), the name is either an heirloom's honest pedigree or a question the campaign might one day answer. The dial is the DM's.
5. **Continuity note:** existing character art shows the gem already red on its diadem — read that as its *final* state, foreshadowed; the item enters play orange.
