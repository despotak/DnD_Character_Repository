# Ar'N'Ehalaer — the Arcstaff of Khavoerm
### *"The Great Staff that is Not"* — 5e translation (Vestige of Divergence)

> **Design contract.** Source: *Cormanthyr: Empire of Elves* (2e, pp. 153–154 — the Arcstaff entry, in full). No Arcstaff has ever been printed in 5e — verified against the current item corpus. The natural 5e chassis is the **Staff of the Magi** (2024 DMG p. 310): the Arcstaff is, recognizably, that staff's ancient elven ancestor — a spell-absorbing, spell-throwing archmage's instrument — with two signatures the Staff of the Magi lacks: it can **throw an absorbed spell back**, and it can **become a dragon**. Powers are translated through that chassis and rationed across **Vestige of Divergence** states (Dormant → Awakened → Exalted); all state transitions are **DM-triggered story beats**.

---

## The lore, in full

### The staff of the first Coronal

When the Rulers' Blade chose **Kahvoerm Irithyl** and made him the first Coronal of unified Cormanthyr (c. −4000 DR), his House began accumulating the regalia of a five-thousand-year dynasty. The greatest arcane artifact among all of House Irithyl's possessions — and thus the Coronal's — is the **Arcstaff of Khavoerm**, named for that first ruler and as old as the realm itself.

Here is the irony the Court lived with for millennia: **no Coronal ever wielded it.** The Irithyl line walked the warrior's path; the staff answers only to a mage's hand. It waited in the **Irithyl vaults** — a hidden chamber whose very existence was known to perhaps half a dozen souls — and only the Coronal and **the Srinshee** knew the command word that summons it instantly to hand from its resting place. The realm's mightiest arcane instrument spent its ages as a *contingency*: the weapon the Court would call for on the day words and warriors both failed.

### The Great Staff that is Not

Its Common name, *Arcstaff*, undersells it; the elven name is exact. Out of a mage's grip, Ar'N'Ehalaer appears to be **a twisted length of lumpy, tarnished metal about four feet long** — vault-clutter, not regalia; a thing beneath a thief's notice.

In the hand of a wizard or High Mage it comes alive. The metal brightens to a **reflective silver sheen** and contorts into an **undulating, wingless, legless dragon** that coils and twists endlessly about **an invisible staff** — the wielder's hand alternately seems to grasp living silver metal or *nothing at all*, though the grip holds true. Around and within the dragon's coils, **sparks and crackles of energy flash brighter and change color when they contact other magic** — a prismatic aurora that reads the Weave around it. The Great Staff that is Not: a staff with no shaft, a dragon with no wings, a weapon that looks like scrap.

### The vault and the fall

The Arcstaff's recorded history is almost entirely a history of *not being used* — which is itself the measure of Cormanthyr's confidence. It slept through the Weeping War; whether any hand called it forth in the city's final hours in 714 DR, no chronicle says.

Then came 1487 DR, and **Thultanthar fell out of the sky onto Myth Drannor** — onto the city, its towers, and its vaults. The Coronal who died knowing the command word died that day's era earlier; the current Coronal, **Ilsevele Miritar, is not of House Irithyl** — the vault-knowledge of a five-millennium dynasty did not automatically pass to her office in exile. One being certainly still knows the word and the way: **the Srinshee**, who appeared amid the city's last hour, saved what could be saved, and vanished again — as witnesses among the survivors can attest.

So the Arcstaff today is one of three things: **crushed** beneath a fallen Netherese city (unlikely — its makers built for the ages), **looted** by Shadovar salvagers who carried off a lump of tarnished metal without knowing what refused to wake for them (delicious), or **still in the vault**, intact beneath the rubble of the Irithyl compound, waiting for someone with the right blood, the right Art — or the right introduction to a sapphire-haired elf who remembers where everything is buried.

*A note for the exiled Court: a realm rebuilding itself at Semberholme has a Coronal with an heirloom sword and precious little else of the old regalia. Returning the Arcstaff to the Coronal's keeping — or being granted its use as the realm's arcane defender — are the same story told from two directions.*

## The item

*Staff, artifact (Vestige of Divergence) — requires attunement by an elf spellcaster*

The staff's active powers are woken by **command words in ancient gold-elf inflection** — the pronunciation itself is the lock. A non-elf attuning to it (if the DM permits attunement at all) finds only the passive coils; the dragon does not answer a mangled tongue. *(2e, verbatim gate.)*

The staff is not sentient, but the dragon-form is *animate* — it has moods the way weather does. It coils tighter in the presence of hostile magic, lifts its head toward strong dweomers, and is known to sulk, minutely, when sheathed in a carrying case.

**Charges.** The staff holds charges with a cap set by its state (10 / 25 / 50). It regains charges **only by absorption** (below) — it does not recharge at dawn. *(Canon: the Arcstaff feeds on the Art; it is not fed by the sun.)*

### ◆ Dormant — "the Tarnished Coil" *(from attunement)*

*The dragon stirs but does not lift its head; the sparks are few and pale.*

- **Quarterstaff, +1** — and any creature the wielder strikes with it takes an extra **1d8 lightning damage** (the coils bite). *(2e constant shocking grasp.)*
- **Feather-light Descent:** the wielder is under a constant ***feather fall*** effect (self only, automatic). *(2e constant.)*
- **The Reading Coils:** the wielder can cast ***detect magic*** at will, and can **identify** a magic item by holding it against the coils for 1 minute (as the *identify* spell, no components). The sparks flash brighter and shift color near active magic even when no power is used. *(2e constant identify + at-will detect magic.)*
- **Light and Dark:** command the staff to shed light (up to bright light, 15-foot radius) or drink it (nonmagical light within 15 feet dims to darkness). *(2e at-will.)*
- **Absorption, first coil:** when the wielder is targeted by a spell of **3rd level or lower** that targets only them, they can use a **reaction** to have the staff swallow it — the spell is negated and the staff gains charges equal to the spell's level (excess above the cap dissipates as prismatic sparks; the staff cannot be overloaded). *(2e rod-of-absorption power, rationed; the canonical "bottomless" version arrives at Exalted.)*
- **Charge powers (Dormant, cap 10):** *hold portal* (1 charge), *knock* (1 charge — the coils pick the lock and the spell's boom is a soft chime), *protection from evil and good* (1 charge).

### ◆ Awakened — "the Silver Serpent Wakes" *(DM story beat; suggested: mid campaign, the staff acknowledged before the Court or the Srinshee)*

*The dragon's head follows conversation now. The sparks run in rainbows.*

All previous, and:

- **+2 quarterstaff.** Charge cap **25**.
- **Absorption, second coil:** spells of **5th level or lower** targeting only the wielder.
- **The Echoed Spell:** when the staff absorbs a spell, the wielder may — **before the end of their next turn** — hurl that same spell back (its original level and effects, the wielder's spell attack/DC) by expending **3 charges**. *(2e signature power, verbatim: the Arcstaff does not merely eat your fireball; it returns it with interest of intent.)*
- **Blast suite** (charge cost = spell level; cast at base level, +1 charge per level to upcast): ***fireball***, ***lightning bolt***, ***wall of fire***, ***wall of ice***. *(2e 1-charge list, converted to the Staff-of-the-Magi cost idiom.)*
- ***Blink*** (2 charges), ***globe of invulnerability*** (6 charges). *(2e at-will blink and 2-charge globe, rationed.)*

### ◆ Exalted — "the Great Staff That Is Not" *(DM story beat; suggested: the realm's arcane defender in deed, 17+)*

*The dragon meets the wielder's eyes. The invisible haft feels, for the first time, warm.*

All previous, and:

- **Charge cap 50.**
- **Absorption, bottomless:** spells of **any level** targeting only the wielder; the staff cannot be overloaded, and if its charges ever run dry, the coils *hunt* — the next eligible spell is absorbed whether the wielder takes the reaction or not. *(2e, verbatim: an empty Arcstaff auto-feeds.)*
- **The Barrage:** ***magic missile*** as if cast with a 9th-level slot (**11 darts**, 2 charges). ***Cone of cold*** (5 charges). *(2e 2-charge list.)*
- **Uncoiling the Dragon (10 charges, once per month):** the silver dragon pours off the staff and grows, within one round, into an **adult silver dragon** (its stat block, maximum hit points; it cannot cast spells and has no lair actions). It is loyal to the wielder and obeys without speech. It remains for up to **4 hours** or until it ends; if the wielder moves more than **300 feet** from it, it shrinks and blinks back into the staff. While the dragon is manifest, **spells cast from the staff may originate from the dragon's space** — the Arcstaff's fire and lightning arriving as breath from a silver throat. *(2e great power, near-verbatim; the leash was 100 yards.)*

## Balance annex

| Power | Prior-edition source | 5e analogue / note |
|---|---|---|
| +1/+2 staff, lightning rider | 2e constant *shocking grasp* (1d6+9 by touch) | Standard vestige weapon ramp; rider ≈ uncommon weapon property |
| Constant *feather fall*, *detect magic*/*identify* at will, light/dark | 2e constant & at-will lists | Utility riders; identify-by-touch mirrors the 2024 *identify* niche |
| Absorption (3rd → 5th → any) | 2e "rod of absorption, no limit" | **Staff of the Magi's own mechanic** (XDMG p. 310), rationed by state — SotM absorbs to +50 from level 1; this reaches parity only at Exalted |
| The Echoed Spell (3 charges) | 2e, verbatim | The one power SotM lacks; priced at triple the absorption's gain, so echoing is a net charge *loss* — a choice, not an engine |
| Blast suite at charge-per-level | 2e 1-charge list | Directly the SotM cost idiom |
| *Magic missile* ×11, *cone of cold*, *globe* | 2e 2-charge list | SotM-comparable expenditures |
| Silver dragon, 1/month | 2e great power | An adult silver dragon (CR 16) for 4 hrs monthly at Exalted tier ≈ a scroll of *summon draconic spirit*'s much bigger cousin — gated behind 10 charges, a leash, and the calendar |
| No dawn recharge — absorption only | 2e economy | The staff is strong exactly when enemies sling magic at its bearer; against sword-and-claw campaigns it starves. Self-balancing by encounter diet |
| Command words elf-gated | 2e, verbatim | Story lock |

**Why a DM can say yes:** every offensive power runs on charges the staff can only earn by *eating enemy spells aimed at its wielder* — the DM controls the fuel supply absolutely. The chassis is a printed 2024 artifact (Staff of the Magi) with its absorption rationed *down* until endgame; the two additions (echo-cast at a net loss, a monthly dragon at Exalted) are paid for by the missing dawn recharge and the missing retributive strike. And narratively it is the *Coronal's* regalia, not the wielder's property — held in trust, summonable away by the right command word in the right mouth, which is an off-switch with five thousand years of legitimacy.
