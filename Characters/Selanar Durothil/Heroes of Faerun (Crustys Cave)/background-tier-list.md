# Background Tier List — for Selanar Durothil

Ranked for fit against Selanar's concept (elven noble academic, House Durothil scion, ex-Akh'Faer, "Seeker of Mysteries," Wizard → Bladesinger duelist, fashion-conscious bon viveur who loves a good mystery).

**Read this as a flavor ranking, not a power ranking.** Per Crusty's house rule ("I allow all players to customize the backgrounds, swapping out skill/tool prof, ASI, and origin feat"), every background's skills/tool/ability allocation/feat can be freely reassigned — so mechanically, any of these converges to whatever we actually want. What *doesn't* fully wash out is the background's name, fluff, and default equipment package, since those carry characterization weight even when the numbers underneath get customized. Ability pool is listed for reference and as a minor tiebreaker (worth keeping as-rolled rather than reshuffling, if it already lines up).

Assumed stat priority for reference: INT (primary) > DEX (secondary) > CON > WIS > CHA > STR (dump). See `build-progression.md`.

## Scope — where this pulls from

The local RAW mirror (`5etools-src/data/backgrounds.json`) has **161 background entries across 26 sourcebooks.** This list covers every entry from the sourcebooks that are actually usable at this table: **XPHB** (2024 core, 16), **PHB** (2014 core + variants, 20), **SCAG** (Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide — FR core, 13), **FRHoF** (*Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn* — FR faction backgrounds in full 2024 format, 18), **BGDIA** (Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus, 14 — mostly city-flavored reskins of core PHB backgrounds, no unique mechanics), **ToA** (Tomb of Annihilation — FR/Chult, 2), **GoS** (Ghosts of Saltmarsh — FR Sword Coast, nautical, 4), plus **Archaeologist**, whose only current reprint lives in an Eberron book (EFA) but which mechanically originated in *Tomb of Annihilation*, a Forgotten Realms adventure, and carries no Eberron-specific content.

**Explicitly excluded as hard-locked to a different published setting** (Eberron dragonmarked houses, Ravnica guilds, Innistrad/Ravenloft, Theros, Strixhaven, Spelljammer, Dragonlance, Planescape, and several Magic: the Gathering plane tie-ins): EFA *(except Archaeologist)*, GGR, RHW, VRGR, MOT, SCC, AAG, SatO, DSotDQ, PSA, PSI, LFL. Also skipped as tonally/mechanically unlisted novelties: ABH, AI, BGG, BMT — none fit "canonical Forgotten Realms, 1495 DR." Full accounting is in the raw source (`backgrounds.json`) if you want to sanity-check the cut.

## Verdict

**Archaeologist** and **Mythalkeeper** are the two strongest finds — both land tighter than the original S-tier pair because they match *specific, already-written* beats in his backstory (the Sharlarion expedition; Myth Drannor's mythal) rather than a general "scholar" archetype. **Archaeologist** edges ahead overall: same INT/WIS strength as the others, but it's the only S-tier pick that also hits DEX, which matters for the no-armor duelist's AC. **Sage** and **Noble** remain excellent, safer defaults if a more generic scholar/noble frame is preferred over the specificity of the top two. **Cloistered Scholar** and **Knight of the Order** (both SCAG, both featuring genuinely useful narrative Features) are worth a look if the 2014-style no-ASI tradeoff is acceptable, given the house rule lets us bolt a modern ability bump onto them anyway.
