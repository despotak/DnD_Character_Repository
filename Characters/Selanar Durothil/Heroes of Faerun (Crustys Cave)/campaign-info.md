## Character creation
- Start at **level 0, classless** — build out during Session 0; built mechanically as 1st-level characters, but class abilities get dropped in narratively as the story justifies them (e.g. a druid's awakening = the trees warning of a threat; a first prayer answered with Cure Wounds)
- **Stat rolling:** 4d6kh3, rolled 5 times; 6th stat = the total sum vs. 75. Roll 3 arrays total — you get access to everyone's unchosen arrays too. Runs slightly above-average, favors MAD builds
- Because stats run higher, lean toward **feats over ASIs**
- Full freedom to customize background skill/tool proficiencies, ASI, and origin feat
- Level 0 feat (and its attached tool proficiency) needs a narrative reason — factor it into the backstory
- All 2014/2024 content permitted, plus starter magic items
- Investigation-heavy game — don't build combat-only, or you'll take a backseat during the mystery/intrigue play
- Don't over-optimize — "a cool character is more fun than an effective character," combats are balanced accordingly
- Hitting level 3 / picking a subclass carries real narrative weight — have a plan for where the build is headed
- Strong character concept + team-focused attitude expected; backstory should include why you started adventuring, personal hooks, and ties elsewhere in the world — "important people in your life will give me more to work with"

## Content boundaries & tone
- No sexual content, whatsoever
- No PvP without pre-consulted consent (any contested roll between players counts)
- Don't be a murder hobo — killing has realistic consequences
- Cooperation over isolation — splitting up is fine, but the party needs a way to find each other when things go wrong
- Gritty themes: corruption, slavery, classism
- Mystery/intrigue heavy, combat can be lethal (higher damage across the board, milestone leveling every 3–5 sessions)
- Miss a session → narrative absence, your character is "off camera" (no combat, rescaled), give the GM a heads-up; chronic absence risks removal

## House rules
- Monster stat blocks heavily modified (resistances/typing/vulnerabilities changed from stock)
- Encumbrance, ammunition, and day-to-day survival aren't tracked — general realism instead
- Shopping happens off-camera unless there's a narrative reason not to
- Material components cost gold; verbal components can't be whispered, somatic can't be hidden
- Long rests require a safe/secure location; short rests as normal; downtime available if space allows
- **Dirty Critical** — beating AC by 10+ counts as a crit (both ways); adamantine armor/weapons banned because of this
- **Dynamic initiative** — rerolled every round (automatic in Foundry); only Alert is reworked (usable Proficiency Bonus times)
- Health potions restore to full, used as an action
- Everyone gets a boon at level 19 regardless of multiclassing; player-specific boons at higher levels
- Attunement slots = Proficiency Bonus; most handed-out magic items are player-specific
