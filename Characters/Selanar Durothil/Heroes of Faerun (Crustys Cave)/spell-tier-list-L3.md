# Level-3 Spell Evaluation — for Selanar Durothil

Two-axis rating of every level-3 Wizard-list spell in the RAW mirror (5etools, pulled 2026-09-08): 58 spells, after dropping the 2014 entries that the 2024 book reprinted. Same scales as `spell-tier-list-L1.md`. Level 2, with the note on what is actually in the book, is in `spell-tier-list-L2.md`. Nyx pass, 2026-11-09.

## The frame

- **Slots.** Level-3 slots arrive at wizard L5: two at L5, three from L6. The free level-3 picks are **L5 and L6, four in all**, competing with any level-2 spell still wanted. Copying from a found scroll or book costs **2 hours and 150 GP**.
- **What arrives with them.** L5 brings **Memorize Spell** (swap one prepared spell on every short rest, so a deep book stops costing a dawn decision) and the lineage's Misty Step. L6 brings **Extra Attack**, with one of the two attacks replaceable by a cantrip. Prepared spells: 9 at L5, 10 at L6.
- **The bar.** A level-3 slot can already buy Spellfire Flare at three blasts (33 if all hit, at Disadvantage in melee), or the Iridescent Blade at 3d8+5 a hit, two hits a round from L6. A level-3 spell has to beat one of those.
- **Concentration belongs to the blade.** Three of the strongest level-3 spells (Hypnotic Pattern, Haste, Slow) are concentration. The real choice at L5–6 is which fights the blade sits out.
- **Signature Spells (L20)** make two level-3 spells from the book always prepared and free once per short rest each, with no casting-time restriction, so Counterspell qualifies. **Song of Victory (L14)** adds a bonus-action weapon attack after any spell cast with an action, which gives action-cast damage spells (Laeral's Silver Lance, Fireball) a rider.
- **Party overlap.** **Counterspell and Haste are on neither the cleric's nor the bard's list.** Cad's and Akir's classes aren't recorded in the build files; unless one of them casts from the sorcerer list (or, for Counterspell, the warlock list), only Selanar can bring them. Dispel Magic, Sending, Tongues, Clairvoyance and Speak with Dead are on both; Hypnotic Pattern, Leomund's Tiny Hut, Slow and Nondetection are on the bard's. Vesper's Knowledge Domain adds always-prepared spells at cleric 5: the FRHoF version gives Dispel Magic, Nondetection and Tongues, the PHB version Nondetection and Speak with Dead. Nondetection reaches the table either way.
- **Table levers.** Long rests require a safe and secure location, which inflates Leomund's Tiny Hut the way it inflated Alarm. Visible casting makes the two big spells with no verbal component, Counterspell and Hypnotic Pattern, the silent ones. Dirty Critical rewards Prone and every other Advantage source. Costly components are real money here: Laeral's Silver Lance 250 GP (kept), Clairvoyance 100 GP (kept), Summon Fey 300 GP (kept), Glyph of Warding 200 GP (consumed), Magic Circle 100 GP (consumed). Heavily modified resistances make force the safest damage type.

## C/D — surveyed, dismissed

**Vampiric Touch** (necrotic melee, the wrong half of the spectrum), **Bestow Curse, Animate Dead, Summon Undead, Summon Shadowspawn**, **Summon Lesser Demons** (hostile demons; a Faerna of Corellon does not), **Life Transference, Stinking Cloud**, **Sleet Storm** (cold), **Wall of Sand, Wall of Water, Tidal Wave, Erupting Earth**, **Melf's Minute Meteors** (bonus actions), **Ashardalon's Stride** (a bonus action and concentration for speed the Bladesong already gives), **Conjure Constructs, Enemies Abound, Gaseous Form**, **Protection from Energy** (Absorb Elements covers it without concentration), **Tongues** (Comprehend Languages reads, and both other casters speak), **Nondetection** (Vesper has it always prepared either way), **Tiny Servant, Linked Glyphs, Antagonize**, **Flame Arrows** (no ammunition), **Fast Friends / Incite Greed** (AI, off-register).

## Recommended: L5 and L6 book additions

**L5 (+2):** **Counterspell** · **Leomund's Tiny Hut** (ritual). If Matt already carries the Hut, **Hypnotic Pattern** moves up from L6.

**L6 (+2):** **Hypnotic Pattern** · **Laeral's Silver Lance**. If 250 GP for the pin isn't there yet, **Syluné's Viper** takes this pick and the Lance waits for a scroll.

**Preparing at L5–6:** Counterspell is the fixed level-3 core. Hypnotic Pattern and the upcast blade compete for the remaining 3rd-level slots by the day's threat, and Memorize Spell lets that call change at every short rest. The Hut never takes a prepared slot.

**Copy when found (150 GP each):** Syluné's Viper → Dispel Magic (first, if nobody at the table carries it) → Haste → Slow → Fly → Sending. Rituals (Phantom Steed, Water Breathing) whenever convenient.

**L20 Signature Spells:** Counterspell · Hypnotic Pattern.
