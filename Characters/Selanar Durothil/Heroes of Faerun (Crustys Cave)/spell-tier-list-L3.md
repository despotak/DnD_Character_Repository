# Level-3 Spell Evaluation — for Selanar Durothil

Two-axis rating of every level-3 Wizard-list spell in the RAW mirror (5etools, pulled 2026-09-08): 58 spells, after dropping the 2014 entries that the 2024 book reprinted. Same scales as `spell-tier-list-L1.md`. Level 2, with the note on what is actually in the book, is in `spell-tier-list-L2.md`. Nyx pass, 2026-11-09.

## The frame

- **Slots.** Level-3 slots arrive at wizard L5: two at L5, three from L6. The free level-3 picks are **L5 and L6, four in all**, competing with any level-2 spell still wanted. Copying from a found scroll or book costs **2 hours and 150 GP**.
- **What arrives with them.** L5 brings **Memorize Spell** (swap one prepared spell on every short rest, so a deep book stops costing a dawn decision) and the lineage's Misty Step. L6 brings **Extra Attack**, with one of the two attacks replaceable by a cantrip. Prepared spells: 9 at L5, 10 at L6.
- **The bar.** A level-3 slot can already buy Spellfire Flare at three blasts (33 if all hit, at Disadvantage in melee), or the Iridescent Blade at 3d8+5 a hit, two hits a round from L6. A level-3 spell has to beat one of those.
- **Concentration belongs to the blade.** Three of the strongest level-3 spells (Hypnotic Pattern, Haste, Slow) are concentration. The real choice at L5–6 is which fights the blade sits out. With Faelyn at the table, fewer of them have to: Faelyn's concentration isn't spoken for the way his is, so Hypnotic Pattern and Slow do as much from Faelyn's hands, and Haste does more, cast on him while he keeps the blade.
- **Signature Spells (L20)** make two level-3 spells from the book always prepared and free once per short rest each, with no casting-time restriction, so Counterspell qualifies. **Song of Victory (L14)** adds a bonus-action weapon attack after any spell cast with an action, which gives action-cast damage spells (Laeral's Silver Lance, Fireball) a rider.
- **Party overlap** (roster in `campaign-info.md`). **Faelyn can take every spell on this list**, so nothing here is Selanar's alone; the question is whose book, and whose concentration, each one belongs in. Of the party, only the two wizards can cast Counterspell, Haste, Hypnotic Pattern, Leomund's Tiny Hut, Slow and Fly, and either can copy the other's at 150 GP. Vesper's cleric list shares Dispel Magic, Sending, Tongues, Clairvoyance, Speak with Dead, Laeral's Silver Lance and Spirit Shroud, and his Knowledge Domain adds always-prepared spells at cleric 5: the FRHoF version gives Dispel Magic, Nondetection and Tongues, the PHB version Nondetection and Speak with Dead. Nondetection reaches the table either way. Cad's ranger list shares Dispel Magic, Nondetection, Protection from Energy and Water Breathing, with level-3 slots from ranger 9, and the Hollow Warden has Phantom Steed always prepared from ranger 9. Akir, in the journal but not the roster, isn't counted.
- **Table levers.** Long rests require a safe and secure location, which inflates Leomund's Tiny Hut the way it inflated Alarm. Visible casting makes the two big spells with no verbal component, Counterspell and Hypnotic Pattern, the silent ones. Dirty Critical rewards Prone and every other Advantage source. Costly components are real money here: Laeral's Silver Lance 250 GP (kept), Clairvoyance 100 GP (kept), Summon Fey 300 GP (kept), Glyph of Warding 200 GP (consumed), Magic Circle 100 GP (consumed). Heavily modified resistances make force the safest damage type.

## S — book locks

| Spell | Source | Mech | Narr | Notes |
|---|---|:--:|:--:|---|
| **Counterspell** | XPHB | ★★★★★ | ★★★★☆ | The 2024 version: the caster makes a CON save, and on a failure the spell fizzles and the action spent on it is wasted (a slot isn't). **Somatic only**, so silent at a table where casting is loud. **Only the two wizards can take it, and two counterspellers beat one:** a reaction each, and a second Counterspell answers the enemy's. At a lethal table, an enemy caster often *is* the encounter, and this is the answer. Reaction tax: on Counterspell rounds, Shield waits. A Mythalkeeper's art is knowing a working well enough to take it apart. First **Signature Spells** pick at L20. |
| **Leomund's Tiny Hut** | XPHB | ★★★★★ | ★★★★☆ | **Ritual: no prepared slot, no spell slot.** Eight hours inside a 10-ft dome that nothing can enter uninvited and that spells of 3rd level or lower can't reach. The "long rests require a safe and secure location" house rule, answered in ten minutes, anywhere, every night. Opaque from outside, transparent from inside, and *"of any color you choose"* (prismatic, obviously). Only the two wizards can cast it, and one casting shelters the whole party: if Faelyn already carries it, this pick goes to Laeral's Silver Lance, and the Hut becomes a 150 GP copy for the night Faelyn is the one who's down. |

## A — strong; book candidates by situation

Every creature in a 30-ft cube that fails a WIS save is Charmed, Incapacitated and at speed 0 until it takes damage or is shaken awake. The fight-ender of level 3. *"A twisting pattern of colors"*: the rainbow, reclaimed, in its most literal form. **Out of S because it does more from Faelyn's hands than from his:** from Faelyn it ends the fight and he keeps the blade; from him, the blade sits that fight out while the party handles what is still standing. ## C/D — surveyed, dismissed

**Vampiric Touch** (necrotic melee, the wrong half of the spectrum), **Bestow Curse, Animate Dead, Summon Undead, Summon Shadowspawn**, **Summon Lesser Demons** (hostile demons; a Faerna of Corellon does not), **Life Transference, Stinking Cloud**, **Sleet Storm** (cold), **Wall of Sand, Wall of Water, Tidal Wave, Erupting Earth**, **Melf's Minute Meteors** (bonus actions), **Ashardalon's Stride** (a bonus action and concentration for speed the Bladesong already gives), **Conjure Constructs, Enemies Abound, Gaseous Form**, **Protection from Energy** (Absorb Elements covers it without concentration), **Tongues** (Vesper and Faelyn can both cast it), **Nondetection** (Vesper has it always prepared either way), **Tiny Servant, Linked Glyphs, Antagonize**, **Flame Arrows** (no ammunition), **Fast Friends / Incite Greed** (AI, off-register).

## Recommended: L5 and L6 book additions

**L5 (+2):** **Counterspell** · **Leomund's Tiny Hut** (ritual). If Faelyn already carries the Hut, **Laeral's Silver Lance** moves up from L6 (or **Syluné's Viper**, if 250 GP for the pin isn't there yet).

**L6 (+2):** **Laeral's Silver Lance** · **Syluné's Viper**. Neither takes concentration, so both stack with the blade. If one of them moved up to L5, this pick goes to whatever Faelyn's book lacks, Hypnotic Pattern first. If 250 GP for the pin isn't there yet, the Lance waits for a scroll.

**Preparing at L5–6:** Counterspell is the fixed level-3 core. Laeral's and the upcast blade share the remaining 3rd-level slots, Hypnotic Pattern comes in on days Faelyn's concentration is spoken for, and Memorize Spell lets that call change at every short rest. The Hut never takes a prepared slot.

**Copy from Faelyn's book or a found scroll (150 GP each):** Hypnotic Pattern → Dispel Magic (first, if nobody at the table carries it) → Fly → Sending, and the Hut if Faelyn took it first. Haste and Slow do more cast by Faelyn than kept in his book. Water Breathing (ritual) whenever convenient; Phantom Steed is Cad's from ranger 9.

**L20 Signature Spells:** Counterspell · Hypnotic Pattern.
