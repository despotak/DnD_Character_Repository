
## Spells reskins, DM's approval pending.

Main reason for the reskins, is to create an `iridescent/kaleidoscopic/prismatic light` theme for Selanar. I'm reclaiming the rainbow from the "alphabet gang".

* *Shadow Blade* -> *Iridescent Blade*
	Theme changed from `shadow` -> `light`
	Damage type changed from `psychic` -> `radiant`
	Conditional advantage changed from `dim light / darkness` -> `light`

* *Green-Flame Blade* -> *Spellfire Blade*
	Theme changed from something that has next to zero lore -> `spellfire`, that has ton of lore
	Damage type changed from `fire` -> `force`

* *Booming Blade* -> *Echoing Light Blade*
	Theme changed from sonic -> to iridescent, kaleidoscopic light
	Damage type changed from `thunder` -> `radiant`

### **Iridescent Blade**

*2nd-level illusion*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You weave together threads of pure, scintillating light to create a sword of solidified brilliance in your hand. This magic sword lasts until the spell ends. It counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient. It deals 2d8 radiant damage on a hit and has the finesse, light, and thrown properties (range 20/60).

In addition, when you use the sword to attack a target that is in bright light, the blade flares with a distracting, kaleidoscopic burst of colors. You make the attack roll with advantage.

If you drop the weapon or throw it, it refracts into nothingness at the end of the turn. Thereafter, while the spell persists, you can use a bonus action to cause the sword to reform in your hand, coalescing from the ambient light.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a 3rd- or 4th-level spell slot, the damage increases to 3d8. When you cast it using a 5th- or 6th-level spell slot, the damage increases to 4d8. When you cast it using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage increases to 5d8.

### **Spellfire Blade**

*Evocation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Self (5-foot radius)
**Components:** S, M (a melee weapon worth at least 1 sp)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You channel raw arcane power into the weapon used in the spell's casting and make a melee attack with it against one creature within 5 feet of you. On a hit, the target suffers the weapon's normal effects, and you can cause an arc of crackling, azure spellfire to leap from the target to a different creature of your choice that you can see within 5 feet of it. The second creature takes force damage equal to your spellcasting ability modifier.

This spell's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the melee attack deals an extra 1d8 force damage to the target on a hit, and the force damage to the second creature increases to 1d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier. Both damage rolls increase by 1d8 at 11th level (2d8 and 2d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier) and again at 17th level (3d8 and 3d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier).

### **Echoing Light Blade**

*Evocation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Self (5-foot radius)
**Components:** S, M (a melee weapon worth at least 1 sp)
**Duration:** 1 round

You brandish the weapon used in the spell's casting and make a melee attack with it against one creature within 5 feet of you. On a hit, the target suffers the weapon's normal effects and becomes wreathed in shimmering, prismatic after-images until the start of your next turn. If the target willingly moves 5 feet or more before then, the after-images collapse into a blinding flash, dealing 1d8 radiant damage to the target, and the spell ends.

This spell's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the melee attack deals an extra 1d8 radiant damage to the target on a hit, and the damage the target takes for moving increases to 2d8. Both damage rolls increase by 1d8 at 11th level (2d8 and 3d8) and again at 17th level (3d8 and 4d8).
