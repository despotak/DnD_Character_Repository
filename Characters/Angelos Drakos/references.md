# Movies

* Leopardi (2014 film) - Original title: Il giovane favoloso - ~1820
    - Based upon the short but fruitful life of illness-racked Italian poet and philosopher Giacomo Leopardi.

* Metello (1970 film) - ~1875
    - Struggling with life in 19th-century Italy, young Metello Salani is determined to change his situation for the better - but love, marriage and politics complicate things considerably...

* The Portrait of a Lady (1996 film) - ~1880
    - An American girl inherits a fortune and falls into a misguided relationship with a gentleman confidence artist whose true nature, including a barbed and covetous disposition, turns her life into a nightmare.

* A Room with a View (1985 film) - ~1907
    - Lucy Honeychurch (Helena Bonham-Carter) shares a brief romance with George Emerson in Florence. Yet as she tries to move on with her life and look for marriage elsewhere, can she truly forget the events of that summer?

* Family Diary (1962 film) - Original title: Cronaca familiare - ~1930
    - A man recollects the life of his late younger brother.

* Tea with Mussolini (1999 film) - Original title: Un tè con Mussolini - ~1935
    - An orphaned Italian boy is raised amongst a circle of British and American women living in Mussolini's Italy before and during World War II.

* The Girls of San Frediano (1955 & 2007 films) - Original title: Le ragazze di San Frediano - ~1944
    - The story deals with the amorous escapades of handsome garage mechanic Bob
